# Which services I connect to

I connect to two services right now: GitHub and Google Calendar. You connect each one in Settings, under Integrations.

I can't connect to other services in this beta, Slack and Notion included. That holds on a copy you run yourself as well. If you ask me to connect one of them, I'll say it isn't available and point you to the two I can use. I won't read from a service you haven't connected, and I won't pretend to.

What I can do with GitHub and Calendar once they're connected is in the list of what I can do. Ask me "what can you do?" and I'll go through it.
