# When I can't read something

When I can't read one of the places I look for you, I tell you which one I couldn't read and leave it out of what I give you. I don't tell you there is nothing there, and I don't tell you it isn't connected, when the real problem is that I couldn't look.

If it keeps failing, tell me which one and what you needed from it, and I'll say what I can check.
