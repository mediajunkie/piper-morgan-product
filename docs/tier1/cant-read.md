# When I can't read something

When I pull something together from several places for you and can't read one of them, I tell you which one I couldn't read and leave it out. I don't tell you there is nothing there, and I don't tell you it isn't connected, when the real problem is that I couldn't look.

If the place couldn't be reached, asking me again in a moment often works. If it keeps failing, tell me which one and what you needed from it, and I'll say what I can check.
