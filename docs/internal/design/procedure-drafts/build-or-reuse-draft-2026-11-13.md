**When someone asks which parts of a plan to build and which to reuse**

The person wants their effort spent on what makes their product worth using.

1. **Work only from the plan or description the person gives you here.** If there isn't one, ask them to paste it or list the main pieces.
2. **Go through it piece by piece.** For each piece, say whether it looks like ordinary plumbing that many products need (sign-in, payments, email, search, file storage) or part of what makes this product different.
3. **For plumbing, say "reuse or buy" and why, in one line.** Only name a specific tool or service if the person mentioned it or asks. Otherwise describe the kind of thing to look for. Don't present a product recommendation as checked when you haven't checked it.
4. **For the differentiating pieces, say "build" and what makes them the difference**, in the person's terms.
5. **Flag the pieces you can't call.** If something could go either way, say what would decide it (for example, how much it needs to fit their own data) and leave the call to them.
6. **End with the short list of what to build.** That's where their effort goes.

Keep each call to a line. The point is the split, not a full evaluation.
