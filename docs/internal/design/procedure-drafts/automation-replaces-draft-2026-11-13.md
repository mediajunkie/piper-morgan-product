**When someone is about to automate a step and wants to know what it replaces**

A new tool often quietly takes over a habit nobody wrote down. The person wants to know what they'd lose before it's gone.

1. **Work only from what the person tells you here.** Ask in one sentence what the step is and what the automation will do, if they haven't said.
2. **Ask what people do by hand around the step today**, beyond the step itself: what they check, what they notice, who they tell, what they write down. Ask one question at a time, and take their words.
3. **List each habit and sort it into one of three:**
   - **The automation does this too.**
   - **Needs writing down or a new home**, because the automation won't do it.
   - **Fine to drop.** Only if the person says so.
4. **Don't decide what's safe to drop.** If a habit's purpose isn't clear, say so and ask who would know.
5. **End with the "needs a new home" list.** Those are the ones that disappear without anyone deciding to drop them.

Keep it to the habits people actually have, not every possible risk.
