Production Plan Map

v0 draft · baseline 2026-10-23 · Exec, merging round 1 from Arch, CXO, PPM and Exec · status: for the four reviewers, then for xian

Bottom line. This map lays PPM's ratified Production plan (230 open issues in 9 sprints plus the Q health lane) over the three audience gates in release-model.md. It marks where each of 42 merged round-1 findings lands: 11 missing, 13 implied, 5 obsolete, 9 misplaced and 4 opportunities. 14 of them were raised by two or more roles. The top three gaps: (1) The Terms (#2062) would promise mechanisms that no issue builds: backups and restore, deletion with a real purge, export, support and incident notice (F1, F2, F3, F6, F7, F14). (2) Public beta can't close in the drawn order: PUB's #1551 needs D's isolation rows, and H's "connected" truth needs C's single resolver (F13, F15). (3) Running Production for outside users has no home: a production environment distinct from alpha, self-serve sign-up and abuse limits, and incident notice (F7, F8, F9).
