### Session Learnings
- **Worktree isolation and Rule 1 are orthogonal mitigations** — the headline cron-lifecycle insight is "promote per failure-mode, not per surface-rule": Rule 1 and Rule 2 read alike but promote oppositely because their failure *timing* differs (REPL-turn-level vs PM-conversation-level).
- **A racing count-check does not catch a concurrent-commit clash** (HOST's 08:05 incident, where Docs's 4-file memo-distribution + 3 MANIFESTs landed under HOST's 1-file cycle-log commit). The race happened *after* the count-check, which is precisely why the fix is architectural (worktree isolation), not more vigilance. Wins the case for the reversal more cleanly than any abstract argument could.
- **Migration model matters**: a session can't self-migrate from Model B to Model A — moving requires an *operator relaunch* in the worktree path (a cron can't `cd` its way out of the wrong working directory). Fresh adopters launching in-worktree unblock directly into Model A and skip the migration entirely (PA evening = the proof). Already-on-Model-B agents (CIO at the time of the convergence) need explicit relaunch.
- **Verify-first kept paying off** at scale: Docs read the May 27 omnibus before amending (it was already accurate); CIO checked the methodology corpus before authoring (would have duplicated 35/36; generalized 36 instead); Arch caught two inventory-drift instances during boundary-map verification; Docs read each GitHub issue/PR body before routing CIO's triage. Generalized cohort-wide in CLAUDE.md "Verify First, Create Second" (`5e2651c37`).
- **Order-of-arrival ambiguity is a real coordination hazard**: PPM got "launch Fire-0 now" (~7:55) and "do not register on main" (~7:53) through different channels in opposite order; resolved correctly toward the cohort directive but the surface signals how fast same-morning ratifications can cross. Worth flagging as an open watch-item, not a one-off.
- **Honest correction as institutional practice**: Lead Dev's documented never-recreate gap ("the wait-default heuristic is safe against over-eager-resume but fails against under-eager-resume") directly shaped the Rule-2 → Model A decision. HOST's three-clashes-in-one-day count-up wasn't excused — it was used as evidence. The cohort's willingness to file the failure modes openly is what made same-day refinement possible.
- **The duty cycle's value lives in the substantive fires, not the cadence** — Docs's 1:46 AM May-27-omnibus filing and PA-evening's 4-fire clean Model-A proof both demonstrate that the cron's job is to *enable* PM-uncompeted overnight/off-hours work, not to manufacture per-fire commits. Exec's logging-convention shift (consecutive clean-IDLE fires don't each get a commit) is the cohort recognizing this.
- **Lead Dev's discovered work is institutional value**: filing Pattern-073 (`AsyncSessionFactory.session_scope()` docstring claims auto-commit but doesn't; latent write-loss risk) while shipping the M2 close shows the discovered-work-discipline working as designed — surface it during the work, not after.
