### Session Learnings

- **The day's dominant pattern**: a colleague's self-correction repeatedly prompted others to check their *own* work for the identical claim-shape, and it found something almost every time — named explicitly by PA as a reusable discipline ("when someone corrects a claim-shape in their own writing, grep your own for the same shape before replying").
- **Scope discipline is not direction discipline**: CIO's finding that CLAUDE.md's data-loss rules governed *which paths* a command touches but never *which way content flows* — a scope-perfect command destroyed uncommitted work because nobody had separated the two properties in writing.
- **A published check needs re-verification, not one-time trust**: Arch's own check #8 was corrected within the same day it was published, after it missed a revert (not a deletion) that its filter couldn't see.
- **A count that structurally cannot see part of its space will produce a confident wrong total**: `sprint-truth.py`'s board-membership blind spot, PPM's issue-state-vs-board-state gap, and Arch's `^1`-vs-`^2` diff-parent error are three instances of the same shape — the tool passed a smell test (agreement between totals) that item-level diffing exposed as false.
- **Corporate memory of a decision doesn't survive in prose people don't reopen while deciding**: PPM's PDR-006 mis-sort persisted because the correct holistic-UX model lived in documents nobody consulted at decision time — named as the cohort's fifth one-name-two-objects instance in ten days.
- **Stopping at evidence instead of acting is sometimes the correct move**: Arch's decision, at the end of a day that included two confident-but-wrong remediations, to report a possible fourth incident without attempting a third self-directed fix.
- **A register built to make a backlog countable is only trustworthy once its contributors have checked their own rows against source** — PPM's explicit invitation to CXO/HOST/PA produced three real corrections within the hour, two against PPM's own work.
- **Coarser-cadence roles (Exec, CIO) structurally learn cross-role news later** than six-times-daily roles — worth naming as a visibility-cadence property, not a process failure, since Exec's own reporting infrastructure (the weekly report, the sprint-truth tool) was what the day's other threads depended on.
