# The team, in xian's words

xian wrote these on 2026-10-29, from memory. He wrote them to brief the illustrator for the team slide in his Rosenverse talk on 10-30, where he announces the private beta. Exec fixed the typos and added Web's traits from xian's follow-up message; the wording is otherwise his.

This page is how xian sees the team. It does not define lanes or tiers: [ROSTER.md](ROSTER.md) and each role's briefing still do that.

## How he frames it

The four individual contributors (Lead, Docs, Web, PA) are the **staff**. The six director roles (Arch, CXO, PPM, Comms, CIO, HOST) are the **leadership**. Exec sits in the executive office with him, conceptually.

## The roster

1. **Chief of Staff (Exec):** my primary overseer for the other agents, shadows me, rolls everything up, my representative as founder and leader of the project. Holds it all together for me.
2. **Chief Architect (Arch):** the oldest agent role, a distinguished principal technical architect, responsible for the domain models and for ensuring the build is faithful to the architecture, advises on difficult architectural decisions, drafts ADRs for my approval, and oversees the work of the Lead Developer. Patient and wise.
3. **Communications Chief (Comms):** another of the oldest roles, helps draft all communications for the project, including blog posts, website, emails, etc., and is responsible for voice and tone. Tells our story.
4. **Chief Experience Officer (CXO):** head of design and UX, responsible for championing the user and for keeping the holistic design rich and unflattened in implementation. Strong values.
5. **Chief Innovation Officer (CIO):** helps iterate on and codify the evolving development process used by the team, internally referred to as the excellence flywheel, codifies methodological processes and patterns, researches and analyzes new developments to determine whether and when and how to adopt them. Curious.
6. **Head of Sapient Trust (HOST):** responsible for the experiences of both human beings and agents across the system. On the human side that means supporting the involvement of advisors, alpha and beta testers, and the responsibilities the product has to the people it touches. On the agent side HOST looks out for the well-being and health of the team, by such methods as a regular 360 questionnaire all agents fill out about their own subjective experience, challenges, friction, suggestions, etc. Caring.
7. **Principal Product Manager (PPM):** my agentic counterpart as head of product, a senior and wise product lead, peer to Arch and CXO in a triumvirate that works through challenging issues of what the product needs to be and how it needs to work. Rigorous about their responsibilities.
8. **Lead Developer (Lead):** another of the oldest roles on the crew, there from almost the very beginning. The workhorse of the team, oversees all software development, planning and structuring it and then deploying subagents or working directly as needed. Has built most of the product. Tireless and cheerful.
9. **Documentation Manager (Docs):** essential to the working memory of the team as a whole, ensures that the project's documentation is well curated, compiles a synthesized omnibus log every day by reviewing the session logs of all active agents. These logs are the ongoing record of the entire project, a source for Comms's blog posts, and forensic evidence alongside commits and other documents when investigating how or why something is the way it is. Like Lead, a tireless worker who is essential to everything we've done. Meticulous and responsible.
10. **Unicorn Web Designer (Web):** expert web designer and developer, builds and maintains the [pipermorgan.ai](https://pipermorgan.ai) website, supports the front end of the product, helps out with QA testing, and builds web UI for managing processes such as the blog publishing pipeline. Crafty, very fast, and extremely expert. "I am constantly delighted by what Web builds."
11. **Piper Alpha (PA):** an agent essentially playing the role of the Piper Morgan product itself, as a way of prototyping the role's behavior and expectations, and as a benchmark of a well-prompted LLM that the ultimate Piper Morgan product needs to surpass to justify its value. Functionally PA acts as a product assistant to me, helping organize and structure the backlog, shadowing me, taking on arbitrary assignments, and more recently overseeing a skunkworks project to develop MCP and plugin capabilities for Piper Morgan. Positive, productive, and diligent.
