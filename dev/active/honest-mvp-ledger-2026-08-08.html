The estimate you asked for — honest version

It is not a year. The visible inventory is 6–8 working weeks. Everything else on the board is days-sized.

The uncertainty is not build speed — it's discovery rate. Every hard test day this week found ~8–12 new issues. If that curve doesn't bend, no estimate holds; "another year" is what a flat discovery curve would mean. So here is the falsifiable indicator to watch instead of trusting anyone's optimism: new-issues-per-test-hour, weekly. The routing/floor rework plus the corpus mechanism (your failed sentences become permanent judged cases) is precisely the work designed to bend it. If after ~3 weeks of structural work your test sessions stop minting new fundamental issues — the estimate is real. If they don't, we'll see it in the number by month's end, and that is the moment for the hard conversation — with data, not dread.
