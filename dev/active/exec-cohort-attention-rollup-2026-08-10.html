The one thing

Discovery ran to 108 issues this week — against a prior eight-week peak of 67 and a last-week figure of 18.

That is roughly six times last week and nearly double the highest week on record, and it happened because you spent real time in the product. It is the clearest vindication of moving the date that exists, and it is a better beta instrument than any open count.

Why the rate beats the count: the open number moves with triage — it fell 26→17 while work grew, then rose to 48 once triage put findings where they belonged. Neither move meant much. The discovery rate measures something the count cannot: whether the product is still surprising you. Beta is ready when that curve falls while your usage holds steady — not when a backlog empties. The tool is development's, scripts/discovery-rate.py, and I'd make it the weekly number you watch.
