# Newsletter / blog post proposal — PM skills

## The angle

**"PM skills for Claude Code — what I built and why"**

The post explains what Piper Morgan skills are: not prompts, not templates, but structured procedures that Claude Code executes as slash commands. The interesting thing isn't that they exist — it's *how* they were built and what they reveal about the future of PM tooling.

## Why this now

- Skills are live and distributable — real thing, not announcement
- The alpha tester email is going out — blog amplifies the signal
- The "what is a skill vs. a prompt vs. a template" distinction is genuinely interesting and underexplored publicly
- Anthropic skill infrastructure is evolving; we're building on the leading edge

## Proposed structure

**Hook**: Something I used to do manually that now takes a slash command. Concrete example — maybe `/stakeholder-update` turning 5 bullet notes into a polished update in 30 seconds.

**What a skill actually is**: Not a prompt saved to a file. A procedure with inputs, outputs, guardrails, anti-patterns, and a quality checklist. The skill runs the whole workflow, not just the LLM call. Show a snippet of what a SKILL.md looks like.

**The 7 skills and what they do**: Brief tour. Resist the list-of-features trap — frame as "what a PM's week looks like with these."

**What's coming**: The plugin (Claude Desktop), profile-aware responses, the full Piper Morgan assistant. Skills are the first piece of something bigger.

**How to get them**: One-command install, link to GitHub.

## Tone guidance for Comms

- PM's voice: direct, no AI-crutch words, comma splices fine, no semicolons
- Show the actual thing, don't just describe it — use a before/after example
- Honest about what's alpha-quality vs. polished
- The "we're building in public" framing fits well here
