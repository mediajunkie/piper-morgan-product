**What actually happened**: all day, every time I ran pytest with `-o addopts="..."` to strip out `--maxfail=1` (so a single failure wouldn't stop a full run), I was *replacing* the entire addopts list, not appending to it — silently dropping the already-present `--import-mode=importlib` along with it. **Corrected #1346 twice** — first down-scoping from "repo-wide bug" to "just the 1 real thing," then confirming even that framing undersold how wrong the original claim was.
