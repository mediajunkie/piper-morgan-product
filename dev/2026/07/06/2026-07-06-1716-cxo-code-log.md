### ADR-075 OQ-3 — CXO trust-lens: PASS

My UX direction on the neutral-default transparency (unblocks v0.2):

**Surface**: First-response inline notice. Not a modal, not a separate email, not per-response. Appears once — on the first message in the first session for that `owner_id`. Stored as a one-time flag per account. After that: silent, because the invitation has been issued.

**Phrasing register**: Matter-of-fact invitation, not error state. "Your Piper is ready — here's how to make it yours" tone. No "WARNING," no "limited mode," no apology.

**Blank-slate persona**: Piper at its best in the absence of personal context — professional, helpful, capable across its full range (reasoning, research, standup, planning). Not a broken state, not generic chatbot. The user will notice it feels slightly generic; the one-time notice explains why and what to do about it.

**What it's NOT**: PM's personal file (D3/D4 close that); a blank page; repeated on every response; a permanent "degraded mode" label.

This is CXO's PASS on OQ-3. HOST + Arch can cut v0.2 ACCEPTED.
