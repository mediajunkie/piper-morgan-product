## Caveats

- Only the PM repo is checked; paths and SHAs belonging to Klatch, One Job, DinP, Mediajunkie etc. cannot be verified here, so non-PM rows are expected to miss and are not evidence of fabrication.
- Attribution is a keyword heuristic on the From line; a PM-attributed insight can still cite another repo's path or SHA.
- `log_hit` is a weak existence test (touched before date). `exists_at_date` is the stronger test but paths are as written in prose (may be abbreviated, relative to a subdirectory, or point to untracked/gitignored files).
- Cited files such as omnibus logs may be written after the event the brief covers; for retrospective briefs a miss at the brief date can mean the file did not yet exist (exists_at_date=None means no PM commit existed before that date).
- Trailing `:LINE` suffixes are stripped before checking.
- Sampling is seeded (20261007) so reruns reproduce, but only if the corpus is unchanged.
