   `user_id` (own `_UNSCOPED_PRINCIPAL` sentinel; legacy 3-arg calls stay unscoped).

   `effective_user_id` into `load_context_state` + `hydrate_turns_from_db`.
5. `web/api/routes/intent.py` — the #731 auto-create block now compares owner on the
