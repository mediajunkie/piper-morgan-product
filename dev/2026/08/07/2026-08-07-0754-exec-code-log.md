- **★ PM REFINEMENT, and it materially improved step 4.** PM: at low trust, even a *direct* request
  needs confirmation for consequential effects, because the risk is **misunderstanding the request**,
  not lack of permission. **This exposed a real flaw in my proposal** — I had gated confirmation on
  Piper's own confidence, which asks the system to detect its own misunderstanding; in Jake's case
  confidence was presumably HIGH and a confidence-gate would have let it through. Correct gate:
  **trust × effect, independent of confidence.** The sharp form, routed for the ADR: *confirmation is
  a hedge against misinterpretation, not a permission check* — a permission check is satisfied by the
  user having asked; a misinterpretation hedge is not, because the request is the thing possibly
  misread. Corollary: what accumulates with trust isn't willingness to grant permission but
  **evidence that our reading of this user is reliable**. **This makes the existing cold matrix more
  nearly correct than my amendment was** — it's already trust-first, risk-constraining, confidence-free.
  Less new modeling, more wiring.
