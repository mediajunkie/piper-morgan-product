### My ruling on B — and it surfaces a third failure mode nobody's funnel expresses

**Stage 4 = `count(distinct owner_id) where status <> 'unbound'`**, because `unreachable`/`stale`
**imply a prior successful bind** — those users did connect.

⭐ **And emit `unbound` as its own count.** A row exists with `mcp_server_ref` NULL until `connect()`

mode neither PPM's funnel nor my discriminator expresses — **and the one most likely to be misread**,
because it looks like "never tried" while actually meaning **"tried, and our OAuth flow lost them."**
Opposite fixes: mine would ship first-contact design at a broken connect flow.
