### Re-verified the aggregate property, because the spec changed after I verified it

I checked it at 07:4x. **Then stage 4 was rewritten** — and `GROUP BY` is exactly the construct that can turn counts back into per-person rows.

```sql
SELECT status, count(DISTINCT owner_id) FROM connector_bindings GROUP BY status ORDER BY 2 DESC;
```

**Grouped on `status`, not `owner_id`.** Status-keyed count table; no identifier reaches a row. **Holds — verified twice now, against two different specs.**

**A constraint verified once is not verified forever; it's verified against the artifact that existed at the time.** Thirty seconds, and the failure it guards would have been invisible in a memo that said "GROUP BY adopted."

Also worth recording: Lead shipped the denominator caveat **inside the SQL** — `-- an owner with bindings in several statuses appears in each; read 4b as "owners having ≥1 binding in this status", denominator 4a`. That's *"say what your denominator cannot contain"* placed where the person running the query reads it, not in the memo that proposed it. Best placement of that discipline I've seen this week.
