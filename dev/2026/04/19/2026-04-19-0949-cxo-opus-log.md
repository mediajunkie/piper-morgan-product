### 2. Colleague Test v2 Formalization

Wrote the updated Colleague Test rubric — the CXO's longest-standing deferred item (recommended April 11, now formalized April 19). Changes are additive, not structural — the R/C/T framework, 7+ threshold, and auto-fail rule are unchanged. Cross-sprint comparability preserved.

Two substantive additions:

**Context dimension refined (2-vs-3 distinction)**: Score 2 is now "uses general domain knowledge" (the LLM being competent). Score 3 is "integrates assembled project context" (Piper demonstrating knowledge of *this user's* situation). This is the difference between "a good LLM" and "Piper" — and it's the differentiator stack's core claim. Fresh-account guidance included: Context 2 on a fresh account is not a failure, but even fresh accounts have trust stage, integration state, and session state available for Context 3.

**Error and degradation path coverage**: The Colleague Test now explicitly applies to fallback responses (LLM down), error messages (action failed), and ethical declines — with worked examples from the UAT showing what passes and fails in each mode. The M1 UAT proved this matters: the canned template fallback scored 1/9 six times because nobody had applied the test to the failure mode. Minimum bar for degradation: no auto-fails (no dimension at 0), even if total is below 7.

Also added: two new worked examples (GitHub pre-flight from Apr 10 UAT, trust query contextual showing 2-vs-3 distinction), single-word input edge case (from #922 finding), and relationship sections for canonical retest, fabrication probes, coherence check, and PDR-004.

**Deliverable**: `colleague-test-v2.md`
