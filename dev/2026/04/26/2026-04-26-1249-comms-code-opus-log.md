### Voice/tone guide application

Applied across all seven new drafts:
- Conversational opening with specific moment and time anchor
- "I"-first authorial presence; named-agent texture for color
- Connectors: "But...", "So here's the thing...", "Mind you...", "The thing about..."
- Italicized stress on key phrases
- Single-sentence paragraphs for emphasis
- Anti-manifesto guardrail explicit in two of the [CONSIDER] notes
- All Pattern, PDR, ADR, and skill references verified against canonical files
- Footer + reader question on every piece

### Voice/tone discoveries during drafting

- The narrative mode benefits from heavier temporal scaffolding (specific times throughout, e.g. "By 7:11 PM Phase A had committed").
- The insight mode is more reflective, less chronological, but still anchors in specific moments rather than abstractions.
- Pair pieces (insight + narrative covering same event) need clear differentiation: insight gives the *principle*, narrative gives the *day*. Verify the Paraphrase / Same Failure exemplifies this; readability test is whether each can be read independently without redundancy.
