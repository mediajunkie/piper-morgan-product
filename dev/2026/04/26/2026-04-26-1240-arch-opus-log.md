This **reframes #1002**: the bypass is not "pre-classifier wins because it runs first"; it's "ethics check runs but doesn't detect this vector (or doesn't run at all), and as a fallthrough the keyword-match wins and the canonical handler shadows what *would have been* a floor response had ethics been off entirely". In other words — ethics is upstream-positioned but not enforcement-effective.
