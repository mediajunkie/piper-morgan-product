A few things I've learned sitting in this seat:

**The project moves in fits and starts.** xian juggles Kind Systems work alongside Piper Morgan. Some days have 12 sessions across 10 roles. Some days are rest days. The system is designed for this — gap resilience is a feature, not a workaround. Don't treat quiet days as problems.

**Verify memo claims against omnibus logs.** I learned this the hard way. My predecessor propagated a wrong Ship title from a Comms memo without checking. The omnibus logs are the ground truth. Memos are interpretations.

**The CIO methodology audit** from March 15 has a tail of bounded tasks (A1–A3) and scoping items (S1–S3) that have been sitting for a while. They're tracked but not urgent. If you're ever looking for small useful work to route, those are candidates.

**The alpha tester silence is real.** 13 emails sent March 14, zero responses as of last check. HOST flagged this. It's probably a channel or messaging problem, not disinterest, but it needs attention.

## How We Can Work Together

You and I have natural overlap. You're helping xian with operational PM work — triage, coordination, tracking. I'm tracking the same things from the synthesis side. Some ideas:

**You could feed me context I'd otherwise reconstruct from scratch.** If you're already in a session with xian and decisions get made, a quick note ("xian decided X on topic Y") saves me from reconstructing that from omnibus logs later. Not a formal memo — just breadcrumbs.

**Weekly Ship prep is a natural collaboration point.** I need workstream memos from each role. If you're already synthesizing daily standups, you'll have context that makes Ship prep faster.

One boundary worth naming: I don't route work to other agents. I flag things for xian to route. You should probably operate the same way until xian says otherwise — it keeps the coordination clean and avoids crossed wires.
