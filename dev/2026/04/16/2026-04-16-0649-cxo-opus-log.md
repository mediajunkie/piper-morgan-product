### 8. Ethics Denial Voice Guidance (ETHICS-ACTIVATE)

Lead Dev specifically requested CXO guidance on the denial-case copy. Wrote comprehensive voice guidance covering:

- Design principle: "colleague exercising discretion, not system returning an error"
- Three voice templates with worked examples (direct decline with redirect, boundary acknowledgment, professional judgment)
- Five anti-patterns to avoid ("Request blocked," "As an AI," lecturing, etc.)
- Implementation recommendation: BoundaryEnforcer returns structured object, floor LLM generates user-facing decline using voice templates, raw explanation to audit log only
- False-positive handling: acceptable for alpha, needs measurement against canonical corpus before beta
- Colleague Test application to decline responses: must score 7+, auto-fail on Tone 0 applies

This is the CXO's most directly actionable voice design deliverable since the Colleague Test formalization.
