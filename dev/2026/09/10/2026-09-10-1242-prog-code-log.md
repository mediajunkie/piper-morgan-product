## Design decisions (pre-implementation)

- **Axes threading**: the confirm-workflow seam threads the CARRIER's registry-declared axes
  (`confirm_pending_action` → DESTRUCTIVE/PRIVATE → NAMED_OBJECT). Per-pending-action axis
  threading (e.g. consent_check on a WRITE×PRIVATE action) would DROP the tier to LOW_CEREMONY —
  that is the WRITE-PRIVATE adoption explicitly blocked on the LOW-tier greedy-row tightening
  (CXO-owned, pinned in test_low_tier_keeps_the_generic_vocabulary_including_its_greedy_residue).
  drafted_issue threads its held action's declared axes (create_issue → WRITE/OUTWARD →
  NAMED_OBJECT, the contract's corrected cell); repo_clarification threads the carrier axes
  (its default-bind is a CONFIRM per #1650).
- **Arm survival per CXO ruling**: CONFIRM tier = no SILENT survival. On STATE_QUESTION the
  seam re-arms ONLY together with a visible restatement of the stored ask appended to the
  turn's answer ("the re-render is itself a new ask, so it ARMS" — §5b). No stored ask →
  nothing to restate → the pop stands (empty-object-slot confirms cannot be accepted, §3).
  READ tier keeps its shipped silent re-arm (CXO: "correctly applied").
- **Restate plumbing**: rides the same two application points as off_topic_prefix (#899):
  the `_apply_soft_offer` funnel + the end-of-method application. Same coverage, same residue.
- **Remaining detect_offer_response calls** in drafted_issue/repo_clarification are SHAPE
  reads (near-accept re-ask discrimination, allow_bare_token), not acceptance decisions —
  their ratchet rows stay, documented.
- **LOW-tier greedy-row residue**: WRITE×PRIVATE seams are NOT in this adoption's scope
  (all four seams sit at NAMED_OBJECT). Pin stays as-is.
