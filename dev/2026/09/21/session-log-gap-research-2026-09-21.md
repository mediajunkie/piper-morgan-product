# Session-Log Gap Research — 2026-09-21

**Author**: Docs (subagent research pass)
**Trigger**: Janus (Design in Product, sibling repo) ran a read-only pass over this repo's session-log history and flagged 29 dates where either an omnibus log exists or real commits landed, but no per-agent session log can be found at any path on the current tree. This document independently verifies that claim against primary sources (live tree + git history on `main`).

**Verified how**: For each date — (1) `ls` on `dev/2025/MM/DD/` and `dev/2026/MM/DD/` at HEAD; (2) a single full-history scan of `main` (`git log main --diff-filter=A --name-only`, restricted to `dev/`, `docs/omnibus-logs/`, `archive/`, `docs-backup/`, plus one unrestricted re-check for the 6 "zero trace" dates) rather than 29 separate `--all` scans — `--all` was tried first and rejected because this repo carries 230 branches, the overwhelming majority ephemeral `worktree-agent-*` refs never merged to `main`, which would have inflated "ever existed" with content that never reached trunk; (3) for every hit, the deleting commit via `git log --diff-filter=D --follow`; (4) omnibus existence via direct path check at HEAD, plus header/body grep for explicit session-log-source citations; (5) commit counts via `git log main --oneline --since/--until` on the literal date string (confirmed the environment's clock and commit timestamps are self-consistent with the fictional-timeline dates — no real/fictional mismatch to correct for). Denominator: all 29 dates Janus listed, 100% checked against primary sources; no date was skipped or guessed.

## Summary

**All 29 dates are confirmed real gaps — zero false positives.** No per-agent session log exists at HEAD or anywhere in `main`'s history for any of the 29 dates. But the *causal story* splits into three very different buckets, and that distinction matters more than the raw count:

- **21 of 29 dates (2025-05-27 through 2025-08-14, plus 2025-06-30/07-01/07-11)** predate the per-agent-log-per-date-folder convention entirely. The earliest per-agent session log anywhere in `main`'s history is `dev/2025/08/15/...` — one day *after* the last of these dates (2025-08-14). Five of these dates (2025-05-27 through 2025-05-31) predate the repo's own first commit (2025-06-01) by days; the "omnibus" for them is a pure post-hoc narrative reconstruction of pre-repo research activity, not a record of anything that could have been committed at the time.
- **6 of 29 dates are genuine zero-trace dates**: no omnibus, no session log, nothing — 2025-06-05, 2025-06-09, 2025-06-13, 2025-06-20, 2025-07-02, 2026-04-20. All 6 had real commits land (1–15 that day). The first five sit in the same pre-convention era as bucket 1. **2026-04-20 is the one modern-era exception** — it sits well inside the current duty-cycle/session-log regime, and a contemporaneous internal audit (`dev/2026/04/21/weekly-docs-audit-2026-04-20-findings.md`, filed the next day) explicitly names it as a known, PM-deferred omnibus gap, not a silent loss.
- **5 of 29 dates (2025-08-16, 2025-08-31, 2025-09-11, 2025-09-12, 2025-09-20) are inside the convention's operating window and still show zero per-agent logs on `main`.** This is the most concerning cluster: the convention existed (had already produced a real log on 08-15), but for over a month afterward nothing per-agent survives on trunk — only omnibus. Two of these omnibus files **explicitly state** they were "Compiled from comprehensive session logs" (08-31) or "Compiled from 10 session logs" (09-11) — i.e., per-agent logs verifiably existed at compile time and are not preserved anywhere in `main`'s reachable history now. For 2025-09-20 specifically, the per-agent logs **do still exist in git** (chief-architect, lead-developer, cursor-agent, doc-mgr logs) — but only on an orphan branch (`remotes/origin/verification/ci-test-1758852617`) that was never merged into `main`.

**Omnibus coverage**: 23 of 29 dates have an omnibus log at HEAD; 6 do not (the zero-trace dates above). All 23 existing omnibus files were added to `main` in one of two bulk operations, both well after the fact: a single "Phase 2 recovery" commit on 2025-09-15 (`a5cd9fd3ef`, covering 2025-05-27 through 2025-08-14) or a bundle inside a 2025-09-26 commit (`7383c9026b`, covering 2025-08-16/08-31/09-11/09-12) plus one 2025-09-25 commit (`367457df1a`, covering 09-20). None of the omnibus headers for these dates read as built live from per-agent logs the way the two explicitly-cited ones (08-31, 09-11) do — most present as narrative/timeline reconstructions with a "Participants" line rather than a cited source list.

**Archive location for pre-mid-2025 logs**: there is **no such location in the live tree today.** `archive/`, `docs-backup/`, and `docs/development/session-logs/` all resolve to "No such file or directory" at HEAD. Both existed historically and were removed:
- `archive/session-logs/` (holding `*-OMNIBUS-chronological-log.md` duplicates of the same 23 dates, added by the same 2025-09-15 bulk-recovery commit) was deleted by commit `7383c9026b` (2025-09-26, "docs: Add September 25, 2025 omnibus session log"). This reads as **deliberate dedup**, not accidental loss — the commit's own diff shows it adding the canonical `docs/omnibus-logs/` copies for nearby dates while removing the redundant `archive/` copies of the earlier-recovered set; the commit message doesn't call out the removal explicitly, so it's bundled/incidental in *labeling* even though the effect looks intentional.
- `docs-backup/` (a broader snapshot of docs/, ADRs, editorial calendar, etc. — not specifically a session-log archive) was removed in commit `f24c9df2e1` (2025-09-02, "refactor: Complete systematic root file organization across 5 batches"), a large deliberate root-cleanup commit unrelated to any of the 29 flagged dates specifically.
- `docs/internal/architecture/archive/` exists today but is a different, current-purpose archive (architecture-doc disposal), unrelated to session logs.

Net: **`docs/omnibus-logs/` is the sole surviving record of this repo's pre-mid-2025 era.** There is no fallback per-agent archive to check.

## Caveats / things I could not cheaply resolve

- I could not determine *why* per-agent logs for 2025-08-16 through 2025-09-12 were never committed to `main` in the first place (vs. committed-then-lost) — the two explicit "compiled from N session logs" citations prove they existed somewhere at compile time, but I found no trace of them ever being `git add`ed to any ref (main or otherwise) in this repo. They may have lived only on local disk / an agent's working directory and never made it into any commit.
- The `git log --all` scan (230 branches, mostly ephemeral `worktree-agent-*`) was abandoned as the primary method because it mixes trunk history with content that never merged — but I did not exhaustively check all 230 branches for the other 28 dates the way I did for 09-20 (found via a name collision while chasing the archive/session-logs deletion trail, not via systematic search). It's possible other flagged dates have similar orphan-branch survivors; I did not have a cheap way to search 230 branches × 28 dates and didn't attempt it. Treat "never existed" for the pre-convention dates as "never existed on `main` or docs/omnibus-logs' source trail" rather than "provably absent from every one of 230 branches."
- I did not attempt to determine who (which role) was active, or what work was done, on the 6 genuinely zero-trace pre-convention dates beyond reading the commit subject lines.
