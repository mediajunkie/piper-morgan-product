  opt-in (no DB touch when off), then identity via the EXISTING `resolve_pm_owner_id` convention
  (`PIPER_PM_USER_ID` → PM Identity section of `PIPER.user.md`; #1260/ADR-071 D7). Fail-closed on
  any error.

  #1320/#1520 refusal family, and deliberately distinct from both ("add your key", never "sign in",
