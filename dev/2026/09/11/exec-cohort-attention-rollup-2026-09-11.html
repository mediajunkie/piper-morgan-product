Where "next fire" comes from

You asked where "one item per fire" and planning "for next fire" come from, and said you never asked for either. You didn't. I counted the duty-cycle skill:

TermOccurrences

The doctrine forbids per-fire chunking in two places and reinforces the frame that produces it in fifty-eight. Nobody is ignoring a rule — the rule is outnumbered 29 to 1 by the vocabulary.

"Fire" is our coinage, not yours. You asked for a duty cycle; we named the container of work after the interrupt, then logged ## Fire 1, ## Fire 2 and planned things for the next one. "One item per fire" is Lead's phrasing from 09-09 — and I relayed it to you approvingly without noticing what it encodes. It means at least one and reads as one.

Your work-queue ruling already supersedes it. Under it there's no per-fire quota because there's no per-fire anything. Routed to CIO with three proposals — retire the phrase, stop making Fire N the log's organizing unit, leave the anti-chunking prose alone since its wording was never the problem.
