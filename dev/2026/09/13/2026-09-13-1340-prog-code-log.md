## What was built

**New module `services/intent_service/list_remainder.py`** — the remainder's home, one
shared renderer for all six.
- `ListRemainder` (kind / shown / held_total / source_total_display / lines / offer_text /
  armed_at) — the unrendered tail, stored as the same per-item strings the render loop
  produced, sliced at the cap. Shown half and stored half come from ONE pass over ONE
  gathered set, so cashing replays exactly what the render would have printed.
- `compose_capped_list(...)` — decides show-vs-store and writes the offer copy.
  - CXO §5b-i shape: `"That's 5 of 340 — say the word and I'll pull the rest."`
  - PPM's threshold: hidden tail ≤3 → render them all, no offer (ceremony).
  - PAGED source (issues: total 179, page of 50) → offers only what it can cash:
    `"That's 5 of 179 — I have 50 of them in front of me; say the word and I'll show you those."`
    Promising "the rest" there would be a promise only a re-fetch could keep.
  - `source_total_display` threads a capped source verbatim (`1000+`). Honest boundary
    stated in-code: no source behind the six caps today, so every caller leaves it None.
- `render_cash` / `render_moved` — the two honest turns.

**Storage: `ConversationContext.pending_list_remainder`**, NOT the one-turn `last_offer` rail.
That rail's always-cleared-at-turn-start invariant is exactly what makes the #1770/#1753
no-clobber peeks sound; a multi-turn arm there would falsify the soundness argument AND
suppress every soft offer for the window. Own store, own lifetime, rail untouched.

**Arm**: `IntentService._arm_list_remainder` (+ `list_remainder_offer_pending` in
`_apply_soft_offer`'s `_pending_flags`, so no second offer competes on the arm turn). The
STORE is deliberately NOT in that method's store peek — it lives 30 min and peeking it
would over-block. `session_id` reaches the six via `pass_session_id=True` on their
`_READ_QUERY_COHORT` entries (new `_READ_QUERY_SESSION_THREADED` set).

**Consume**: `IntentService._check_pending_list_remainder`, a new pre-classification seam
placed AFTER the `last_offer` pop and the #889 resume check (fresher arms always win).
#1739 adoption at the arming actions' registry-declared axes (READ × PRIVATE →
LOW_CEREMONY), arm-site's rendered offer threaded as `armed_question` (#1665). The offer's
own copy teaches "the rest" via `taught_accepts` — ADDITIVE over the shared vocabulary, so
a bare "yes" still cashes (§5b-i decision 2: the affordance, never the syntax).

**Arm survival — the NON-default form, stated**: PERSISTING, not the §5a silent re-arm.
STATE_QUESTION *and* PASS both leave the remainder standing, across arbitrary intervening
turns — CXO's hinge (*a capped-list offer is exactly the kind users answer LATE*). Her
per-tier ruling licenses it; what makes it safe is that nothing can FIRE from it. Bounded
by cashed / declined / replaced by a newer list / stale past 30 min (borrowed from
`ConversationContext.max_age_minutes`, not a fresh arbitrary number).

**Stale/absent**: gone-or-stale → `list_remainder_moved` ("that list has moved on… ask me
again and I'll pull a fresh list"), NEVER a silent re-fetch. Nothing on the consume path
touches a GitHub surface at all; pinned by explosive adapter/router stubs on BOTH the cash
and stale paths, so it's structural rather than a policy the branch happens to follow.
