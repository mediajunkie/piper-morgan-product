### The shape, and four decisions with their reasons

> ✅ **"That's 5 of 340 — say the word and I'll pull the rest."**

⭐ **"5 of 340", not "…and 335 more"** — same fact, but **the first tells the user what they're HOLDING**
and the second what's missing; **§2's rule applied to display.** ⚠️ **A remainder count also invites
subtraction nobody asked them to do.**
🔴 **Offer the affordance, never the syntax** — *"say the word"*, not *"say 'show me the rest'."*
⚠️ **If only one phrasing works, that's an acceptance-contract defect, not something to document at the
user** (the #1579 and #1108 failure).
🔴 **The count carries provenance discipline**: GitHub caps some counts, so **if the source says `1000+`
we say `1000+`.** ⭐ **A cap rendered as an exact number is a fabricated denominator, arriving through the
one field that looks purely mechanical.**
🟡 **No offer when the remainder is trivially small** — **a cap that hides two items then offers to
reveal them is ceremony.** *(Threshold is PPM's.)*

### 🔴 The constraint nobody had named

⚠️ **A capped-list offer is exactly the kind a user answers LATE** — read the five, think, come back.
📄 **But arms live exactly one turn** (verified 09-09). **An intervening turn kills it and the later
"yes" lands on nothing — #1694's felt shape, manufactured deliberately.**

✅ **My own tier ruling already licenses the fix** — *arm survival is per-tier; READ/COLLABORATE may
survive; only CONFIRM must not.* ⭐ **A list read is the cheapest tier there is, so this is both the case
that needs survival most and one already admissible.**

> 🔴 **Therefore epic 6's acceptance test needs the LATE follow-up.** ⚠️ **Arch's stated test —
> "…and 335 more" → asks → returns them — passes WITHOUT exercising the property that actually fails.**

⭐ **That's the fire's real contribution: the answer to the question asked was four copy decisions; the
useful part was a test-design consequence that came from cross-reading two of my own contracts.**

### And one copy consequence of Arch's rule

📄 *"RETURNS them, from the outcome, not a re-fetch that might disagree with the claim."* **If the stored
remainder is gone or stale, the honest turn says the list moved and offers a fresh read.** 🔴 **A silent
re-fetch presented as "the rest" is a fabrication of continuity** — the user believes they're holding
items 6–340 of the list they saw.
