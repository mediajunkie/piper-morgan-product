### 23:20Z — DIAGNOSIS: the problem statement was inverted, and the cause is the
### measuring environment, not the code

**The numbers I was handed are LOCAL numbers, not CI numbers.** They came from
`scripts/run-sweep.sh ratchets`, which runs the gate under `venv/bin/python` —
the 228-package dev venv. CI runs it under a 15-package pinned venv. The two
environments disagree by **75 errors**, because `ignore_missing_imports = True`
turns every unresolvable third-party import into `Any` and silently suppresses
every downstream error in that module. The dev venv resolves notion-client,
chromadb, slack-sdk, google-api etc.; CI does not.
