# piper-stakeholder-update

A Piper Morgan skill that writes stakeholder updates calibrated to your audience.

## What it does

Takes rough notes — what shipped, what's blocked, what's next — and produces a clean stakeholder update calibrated for your audience: exec, team, investors, or board. Draws on your project context for voice and detail level.

## Trigger phrases

- "write a stakeholder update"
- "draft an update for"
- "what should I tell [audience]"
- "write a status update"
- "help me update [exec/team/investors]"

## Requirements

Works with any level of raw input — bullet points, rough notes, or just a conversation.
