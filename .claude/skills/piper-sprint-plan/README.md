# piper-sprint-plan

A Piper Morgan skill that scopes a sprint from your backlog.

## What it does

Given your open issues or a manually-supplied backlog, this skill helps you decide what to pull into the sprint, what to leave out, surfaces dependencies and risks, and produces a proposed sprint with rationale you can accept, adjust, or reject.

## Trigger phrases

- "let's plan the sprint"
- "help me scope [sprint name]"
- "what should go in this sprint"
- "which issues should we tackle next"
- "sprint planning"

## Requirements

Works best with a GitHub-connected project. Also works with a manually-pasted backlog.
