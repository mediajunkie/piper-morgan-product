## Communication Dynamics

### Session Patterns (Code-era operating cadence)
- **Lead Developer**: Daily; near-daily shipping spine (Tier 1)
- **Documentation Management**: Daily; omnibus synthesis + publishing pipeline (Tier 1 cadence by daily output)
- **Chief Architect**: Weekly+ (Tier 2); pattern decisions, ADR creation, soundness reviews
- **Chief of Staff**: Variable; tracker reconciliation + Weekly Ship synthesis + handoff review (Tier 1 by responsibility)
- **Communications Director**: Weekly; editorial strategy + Ship/building/insight content
- **PPM**: As-needed (Tier 2); sub-epic gates + PDR work + quality thresholds
- **CXO**: As-needed (Tier 3); Colleague Test discipline + UAT
- **CIO**: As-needed (Tier 3); methodology codification + pattern stewardship
- **HOST**: As-needed (Tier 3); cadence keys to PM bandwidth — leadership altitude, ground-truth access on demand

### Coordination Channels (Code-era infrastructure)
- **Mailboxes**: `mailboxes/{role}/{inbox,read,sent}/` — direct filesystem access; mailbox writes commit to `main` (Apr 26 norm)
- **Session Logs**: `dev/active/` during session, `dev/YYYY/MM/DD/` at close
- **Omnibus Logs**: `docs/omnibus-logs/YYYY-MM-DD-omnibus-log.md` — daily synthesis, Step 2.5 + 2.6 cross-reference gates
- **Workstream Reviews**: Friday–Thursday windows, role-scoped memos to Chief of Staff
- **Weekly Ships**: CEO + Exec synthesize from workstream reviews + omnibus; Comms drafts narrative; CEO voice-pass; publish Wednesdays
- **GitHub Issues**: Technical tracking + audit-cascade gameplans + role-health-check artifacts

### Escalation Paths
- Technical blockers → Chief Architect
- Resource/priority → CEO (xian)
- Process/methodology → CIO
- Agent coordination → HOST (role-health) or Chief of Staff (operational)
- Product decisions → PPM (review-gates 5-class surface per May 4 proposal, CEO-approved May 10)
- Voice / experience → CXO
