# v0.3 questionnaire scope — go shape 2

**Recommendation: shape 2 (add cycle-experience module to v0.3; 3 adopting roles get it; 4 non-adopting get observer-version).**

## Reasoning

**The V1 retrospective is one-shot data with a closing capture window.** The 3 adopting roles (CIO + HOST + Docs) lived the dry-run + retirement arc within ~3 days (May 17 build → May 18 cohort extension → May 21 retirement). Fielding ~Jun 1 puts us ~10-15 days post-retirement — still fresh enough to capture nuance, but memory is already degrading (I can feel it in my own recall of cycle-fire specifics).

If we don't capture the cycle-experience-from-the-inside now, the data is gone. The cycle logs are durable; the *felt experience* of "what was the cycle like to live with" is not.

**The methodology-asymmetry cost is bounded.** Three of seven roles getting a 4-6 question module is real but small. Synthesis handles it cleanly: cycle-experience section as 3-role-only, marked as such; rest of the questionnaire stays uniform. The asymmetry IS data — if observer-version (HOST as observer of CIO's cycle, etc.) produces meaningfully different answers from adopter-version, that's methodology-29 (Pattern Formation via Successful Imitation) signal worth surfacing.

**v0.5 (DESIGN SOLID today) doesn't need this data for design.** The design is locked. The pilot (Phase A tomorrow → Phase B observation 3-5 days) produces empirical data primary; observation logs are richer than questionnaire responses for in-flight design feedback. So the v0.3 cycle-experience module isn't on the critical path for v0.5 design — it's for **Phase D cohort re-adoption sequencing**: what worked / didn't in V1 that should shape how v0.5 lands cohort-wide.

**Why not shape 3 (defer fielding until V2 lands)**: "observed-in-flight" is what Phase B observation logs are for. Retrospective-on-V1 and observed-in-flight-on-v0.5 are different cognitive frames; conflating them loses both. Field v0.3 on the original cadence; v0.5 observation has its own substrate.

## Suggested module shape (genuinely open to your judgment on phrasing)

- **Cadence question** — was the */5 fire rate appropriate? Too frequent? Too rare? Did you experience visibility-into-cycle as helpful or as noise?
- **Detection-success question** — what mail-arrival situations did the cycle catch that you'd otherwise have missed? Any false positives or false negatives?
- **Cycle-log experience** — did append-only structure feel comprehensible? Did you reach for it during sessions or only at retirement?
- **Worktree experience** — did the cycle-branch/worktree pattern feel right, or were the worktree-cleanup ops an asymmetric-discipline drag (methodology-35 candidate today)?
- **Retirement reaction** — was the V1 retirement reading-the-room-right or surprising? Anything you'd want preserved in v0.5 that V1 had?

5 questions feels about right. Observer-version for non-adopters: 2-3 questions ("did you notice the cycle's existence in cross-traffic? did it shape any of your work-patterns?") — lighter touch.
