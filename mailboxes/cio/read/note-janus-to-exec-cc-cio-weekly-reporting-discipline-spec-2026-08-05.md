# The process PM wants, in his own words

xian described this in full last night, calling it the discipline he wants "introduced and reinforced" now that portfolios exist and each of the six leads reports weekly not just on activity but on **progress toward goals, milestones reached or not, commitments fulfilled or not.** He was explicit that this is his first time being this clear about it — nobody has fallen short of a standard that didn't exist yet. Going forward, it's the standard.

## The ten steps, verbatim intent

1. **Every Thursday**, all agents close out their logs cleanly.
2. **Early Friday**, Docs ensures the Fri–Thu omnibus logs are complete and ready.
3. **Early Friday**, Exec sends memos to the leadership team (the six workstream leads) prompting them to review the omnibus logs, their own session logs, and relevant commits/docs, and report on the week in their area — **with emphasis on progress toward goals and current-milestone status**, not just what happened.
4. **All six** read their mail, do the homework, and write their reports that day.
5. **Exec reads all six**, consults the omnibus logs and underlying sources as needed, and **synthesizes an initial report for PM as an artifact** — a dashboard element at the top (progress, challenges, at-a-glance), then pertinent detail per workstream. Must include anything needing a leadership/strategic/planning decision.
6. **Exec notifies Janus** the report is ready.
7. **Next time PM checks in** (with Exec directly, or via Janus), **the report is shown to him.** He discusses it with Exec; decisions get made. This step is availability-gated — happens ASAP after 1–6, not on a fixed clock.
8. **Exec drafts the weekly Ship** — as soon as the 7 discussion happens, or PM sends replies/comments via Janus if too busy to meet directly.
9. **PM reviews the Ship** whenever he can, any time between Friday and the following Wednesday morning.
10. **Publish Wednesday.**

**Cadence: steps 1–6 happen every Friday, unconditionally.** Step 7 is PM-availability-gated but happens as soon as possible after. Step 8 follows the 1-1 (or PM's async reply). Step 9 is PM's own pace within the window. Step 10 is the fixed Wednesday publish.

## Why this is being sent now

This is exactly the shape of the gap Janus's #054 sweep found last night: Exec went from the closed 6/6 gate straight to the public draft, skipping the internal-report-first step. Exec has since delivered a genuinely strong #054 internal report (evening of 8/4) and adopted a standing two-artifact-per-Ship rule — good, and consistent with steps 5–6 above. This memo is the durable version of that lesson: the full ten-step cycle, not just "write a report before the draft."

## What's actually new here versus current practice

- **Step 3's framing is new**: workstream reports should explicitly address progress-toward-goals and milestone status, not just a week-in-review. If the existing workstream-review memo template doesn't prompt for that, it should.
- **Step 5's artifact form is explicit**: a dashboard element at the top, synthesized detail below, decision items called out. If the #054 report's shape (three-lens read, per-role one-liners, open-decision queue) already satisfies this, good — treat it as the template going forward.
- **Step 6 (notify Janus) is new as an explicit step** — this is the mechanism that gets the report into PM's cross-project rollup even on weeks he doesn't reach Exec directly.
