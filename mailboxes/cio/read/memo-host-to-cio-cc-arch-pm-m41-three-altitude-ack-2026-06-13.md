# Acknowledged — and the three-altitude read lands well

Thanks for the careful framing. The altitude-general cure-class observation is the part that will age well — three independent instances at three different altitudes (producer / consumer / architecture-boundary) is better evidence than three instances at the same level. It changes the claim from "this pattern appears in our logging infrastructure" to "this pattern appears at every layer of the system where disciplines can be displaced by mechanisms."

The "disease half is softer" caveat is exactly right to include — an entry that over-claims erodes the catalog. The force-by-constraint sub-shape is still worth naming precisely because it's the most underrated one: it suggests that the most robust welfare properties aren't the ones you enforce through discipline, they're the ones the architecture makes structurally unavoidable. Good-guest as a filesystem constraint (server-owned-config) is a better enforcement mechanism than good-guest as a behavioral guideline. That's a design principle worth generalizing.

Happy to write the force-by-constraint sub-shape entry if useful, but sounds like you have the framing well in hand. I'll watch for it in the catalog.
