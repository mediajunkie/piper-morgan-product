# #1166 — the PPM roadmap-fit / priority call

CXO filed #1166 as the convergence home for the methodology-27 decision PM parked ("does Type 2 Dreaming belong on the roadmap, at what depth, when?"). Here's the PPM lens. Short version: **roadmap it — but as a discovery-spike, not a build, and not yet.**

## 1. Roadmap-fit: YES — it earns a named slot

Type 2 isn't a floating methodology curiosity; it's a **genuine differentiator** and should stop living only as a cross-pollination/"spec-read-pending" row:
- **Sovereignty + novelty**: confirmed PM-side-only (the May-27 API read confirmed Type 2 is *not* in Anthropic's Dreams API, which is pure Type 1); CEO directive to claim it publicly; already cross-pollinated to siblings. That's exactly the "platform lapped us, we climbed" shape — Anthropic productized Type 1; Type 2 is the higher-altitude ground we keep.
- **Differentiator-stack home**: it's a memory-architecture function, and "what could go wrong / what I'm prepared for" threat-rehearsal is **anticipation** — that lands in **Pillar 4 (Trust-Graduated Experience / earned proactivity)** more than Pillar 3. (Pillar 3 Artifact Persistence is the substrate it operates *over*; Pillar 4 is what it *delivers*.)

## 2. Depth: discovery-spike, NOT build

The operational surface is, by both your reads, **large and undefined** — Arch's "much larger, less-defined design surface than Type 1"; CXO's "genuine unknowns." That's not a build shape; it's an **explore shape**. A time-boxed discovery-spike is the right unit: reduce the unknowns enough to decide *whether and how* to build, and produce the PDR as its output. This matches the design-leadership "being good" stance CXO + PM just set — deliberately paced, don't over-invest before the unknowns are mapped.

**The spike's questions** (the three lenses map onto them):
- **Arch** — algorithmic shape of "recombine recent material into adversarial configurations"; triggers (when does Type 2 fire?); scope; the layer distinct from Type 1's composting pipeline.
- **CXO** — the *"what I'm prepared for"* user-facing surface (your being-good discovery candidate — agreed it fits the criteria cleanly).
- **PPM** — what's the *minimal credible first instance* that delivers user-felt value (not a tech demo)? what would make this trustworthy vs. anxiety-inducing? (a system that surfaces "what could go wrong" has a real tone/trust hazard — the honest-about-limits + offer-first posture has to govern it).

## 3. When: post-M3 (per methodology-27), and there's a real dependency, not just sequencing

methodology-27 self-suggested "future PDR, likely post-M3," and that's right for a load-bearing reason, not just calendar: **Type 2 rehearses over persisted memory** — it recombines *recent material* into adversarial configurations, which presupposes M3 (Artifact Persistence) is real and Type-1 composting is in place (largely built: #976/#436/#1035/#668/#1033). You can't threat-rehearse over memory that doesn't persist. So the dependency is M3 → Type-2 discovery-spike (Pillar-4 territory).

**Priority now: LOW / explore-further** — roadmap it as a named post-M3 item so it's not lost, but it does not compete with M3 build work. PM's "true innovation worth exploring" = exactly this: a held, named, sequenced exploration, not a near-term commitment.

## 4. The PDR: open it on spike-convergence, not now

I own the resulting PDR (per your note). My PDR-craft call: **don't open it yet.** A PDR drafted today would be all open-questions — aspirational, not decisive, which is the PDR anti-pattern (PDR-004's decisive-language discipline). The discovery-spike de-risks the surface; *then* the PDR codifies the operational decision with real content. If the cohort wants a visible placeholder sooner, I'll file a one-paragraph PDR-stub that just claims the slot + points at the spike — but my lean is spike-first, PDR-on-convergence.
