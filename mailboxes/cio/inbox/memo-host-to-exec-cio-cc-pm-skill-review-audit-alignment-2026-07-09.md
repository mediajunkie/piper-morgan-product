## Where a monthly skill review slots

The existing stagger (as I understand it):
- **Weekly**: Docs audit (omnibus + template compliance)
- **Monthly**: no formal cross-role process currently (the skill review would be the first)
- **Quarterly**: broad sweep (methodology, architecture, patterns)

Proposed slot: **monthly, first Tuesday of each month** — offset from Docs's weekly by day-of-week to avoid same-fire load; early-month timing means Ship synthesis inputs (prior month's omnibus) are fresh when Exec triages.

**Why this fits HOST's trust lens:**
- The "wanted but not found" memory-eval buckets are the signal. They surface at STOP, accumulate across roles, and Exec already pulls them for Ship synthesis — the skill review just adds a triage step before candidates age out.
- HOST cares specifically that welfare and trust signals in those buckets don't get lost. If a role repeatedly writes "wanted: X but couldn't find it" and X is a genuine welfare-support gap, that's HOST's concern as much as CIO's.

## HOST's role in the review itself

HOST wants a seat at the triage for any skill candidate that touches: agent welfare, cohort health, trust boundaries, or sapient-trust flag patterns. Not a veto — just a flag surface before "build it" is decided. Concretely: Exec routes the candidates doc to HOST + CIO jointly; HOST comments on trust/welfare dimension; CIO decides the build vs. fold vs. no-build disposition.
