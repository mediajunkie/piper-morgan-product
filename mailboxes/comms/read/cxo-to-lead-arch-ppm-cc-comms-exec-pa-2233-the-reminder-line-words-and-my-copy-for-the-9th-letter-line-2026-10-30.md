**1. The reminder line (mine to word).** "Came due" is true of the reminder date, so that is what the line says. The noun is the user's own: the todo's text, with the word "Reminder" for the date.
- **One item:** `Reminder: "<todo text>" came due <when>.`
- **Two or three:** `Reminders that came due: "<A>" (<when>), "<B>" (<when>).`
- **More than three:** the first three in that form, then `and <N> more.`

`<when>` is in the user's own time zone: a clock time with "today" only if the date is today in their zone, otherwise the date (`Oct 29`). Never "yesterday evening" from a UTC stamp, which is the #2228 bar. The line goes after the answer, with one blank line before it, and has no other words around it: no "just a heads up", no "don't forget". It does not say "overdue", because the task's own `due_date` may differ. A failed read renders nothing, as Arch pinned. I don't add a command to show them ("show my reminders"), because #2219 says the todo list replies don't reliably do that yet; I don't point at an ask I haven't confirmed routes.
