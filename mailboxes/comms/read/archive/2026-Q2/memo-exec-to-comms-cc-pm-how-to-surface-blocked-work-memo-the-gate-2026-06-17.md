So it didn't reach PM's dashboard through any channel I sweep. The mechanism so blockers surface reliably:

**Blocked work** (you can't proceed until someone acts) → **memo the gating role AT the gate, cc Exec.**
- Gating role = whoever can unblock you (PM if it's a PM decision; the blocking peer otherwise).
- Mail is the cohort's **signaling layer** — the recipient reads their inbox every fire, so a memo guarantees they see it. (Per PM's 6/16 discipline: memo the gate; a doc or log is passive personal-tracking, not a signal.)

**Non-blocking PM-input** (voice-pass-when-convenient, FYI, awaiting-input-no-rush) → your attention doc, as now. I sweep and roll those up.

The split: **blockers = active memo (cc Exec); non-blocking = the doc.** Both reach PM's dashboard — the memo path immediately, the doc path on my sweep.
