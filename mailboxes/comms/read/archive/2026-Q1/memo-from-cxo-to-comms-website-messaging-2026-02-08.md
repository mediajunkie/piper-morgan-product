The website is transitioning from "learning project, follow the journey" to "product you can try." The primary CTA becomes user acquisition (alpha signup → beta waitlist → eventual sign up/download). 1. **Try Piper Morgan** (Primary) — Branches to alpha signup or beta waitlist based on user appetite
2. **Get Involved** (Secondary) — Contributor path → pmorgan.tech
3. **Learn More** (Tertiary) — Journey, methodology, Playbook

## Core Insight (Our Differentiator)

> **PM tools assume work is items in lists. But PM work is actually relationships between concerns at different scales.**

This is what we understand that task managers, project trackers, and generic AI assistants miss. PM work is inherently multi-scale, multi-context, and connective. Piper is built for that reality.

---

## What Resonated

### Value Proposition Direction
> "Think bigger. [Something about comprehensive awareness]. Piper holds the [your world / what matters / the threads] so you can focus on the decision."

**What works**: Amplification framing (same you, more capacity). Outcome-oriented. User benefit centered.

### Positioning Line
> "Your AI colleague for the work between the work."

**What works**: Names the gap other tools don't address. The connective tissue. The meta-work of PM.

### Supporting Insight
> "Piper holds the context so you can focus on the decision."

**What works**: The sentiment — Piper handles cognitive load so you can think at the level that matters.

---

## What Needs Refinement

### "Track everything"
**Problem**: Too generic. Sounds like any project management tool.

**Intent**: With Piper you can stay on top of everything, zoom your attention to any aspect with relevant background, respond to circumstances with appropriate awareness. It's about comprehensive situational awareness, not task tracking.

**Exploration needed**: Language that conveys "nothing falls through the cracks" + "always have the full picture" without sounding like a todo app.

### "Context"
**Problem**: LLM term of art. May confuse or feel jargon-y to general audience.

**Intent**: The background, the connections, the history, the relationships between things — what you need to know to make good decisions or respond intelligently.

**Exploration needed**: Plain-language alternatives. Possibilities to explore: "the full picture," "what matters," "your world," "the threads," "the background," "what you need to know."

## Thematic Guidance for All Copy

| When writing about... | Emphasize... | Avoid... |
|-----------------------|--------------|----------|
| What Piper does | Connections, decisions, awareness | Task management, productivity metrics |
| How Piper helps | Amplification ("you + Piper") | Replacement ("Piper does it for you") |
| Piper's personality | Colleague, growing, earning trust | Assistant, tool, servant, partner |
| Differentiation | Relationships vs. lists, multi-scale thinking | Feature comparisons, "better than X" |
| The journey | Transparency, systematic methodology | Marketing polish, hype |
| User benefit | Focus on decisions, think at the right level | Get more done, save time |
