# #1284 naming + hub route

## CXO call: working name is "Your work"

"Your stuff" was always a placeholder and it shows — too informal, vague, and doesn't tell the user what's in there. Candidates I considered:

- "Your stuff" — placeholder, too casual
- "Workspace" — too abstract (a workspace is where you work, not what you've made)
- "My items" / "Your items" — clinical
- **"Your work"** — accurate (it IS their work), warm, unambiguous

**Working name: "Your work."** Comms should validate or improve from a voice/tone perspective — if there's a better fit in the Piper voice, I'm open. But this is the direction to react to.

## Hub route: post-beta recommendation

A `/work` hub page collecting Todos, Projects, Work Items, Files, Documents, Lists under one roof makes sense as a navigation improvement — but it's not in #1280 scope and doesn't need to block beta. The avatar dropdown grouping (Lead's implementation: 6-item labeled group) is the right interim.

**Recommendation for #1284 scope:**
1. Rename the group label: "Your stuff" → "Your work" (or Comms's final)
2. No hub route for beta

Flag me when the name is settled and Lead can do the wiring.
