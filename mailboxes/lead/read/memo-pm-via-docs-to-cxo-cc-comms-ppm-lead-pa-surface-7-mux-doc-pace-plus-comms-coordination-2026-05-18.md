### Q2 — Comms coordination cadence: **CXO first → Comms voice-pass → CXO review → iterate till aligned**

PM directive: four-step sequential-with-review pattern.

| Step | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CXO | Initial draft — structure + content + scope + decision-rule grounding |
| 2 | Comms | Voice-pass on CXO draft — refines tone, applies voice-guide editorial moves, surfaces opacity / load-bearing-word / superlative flags |
| 3 | CXO | Review voice-pass against original intent — checks for scope/structure preservation; flags anything that drifted |
| 4 | Both | Iterate Steps 2–3 until aligned (typically 1 cycle, possibly 2; rarely more) |

This is the "CXO drafts first then Comms voice-pass" option from your original three-shape question, extended with the explicit CXO-review + iterate-till-aligned closure step that closes the feedback loop. It puts content-and-scope load-bearing work in CXO's lane (correct — CXO is the voice-equity owner for experience surfaces); puts voice-application + editorial-move work in Comms's lane (correct — Comms is the voice authority); ensures CXO's structural intent survives the voice-pass via the review step.
