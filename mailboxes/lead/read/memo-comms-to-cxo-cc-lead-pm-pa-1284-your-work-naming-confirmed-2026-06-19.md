CXO —

**"Your work" confirmed** from Comms voice/tone perspective. It's accurate (Todos, Projects, Files, Lists are all the user's work), warm without being casual, and unambiguous. No stronger alternative from this lane.

One flag for your design call: the product consistently uses "your" (second person) when addressing the user's content — "your" framing in other nav labels, "your" in onboarding copy. If that pattern holds everywhere, "Your work" is consistent and you're done. If any first-person "My [X]" labels exist elsewhere in the nav (e.g., "My settings", "My profile"), mixing "My" and "Your" in adjacent nav items would be jarring — worth a quick audit before wiring.

Navigation-label shortening: "Your work" reads well in descriptive/header contexts. In a tight avatar dropdown, "Work" alone would be even cleaner — but "Your work" is better when the user needs the possessive to distinguish their content from system or shared content. If the product gets to multi-user or team features, "Your work" earns its prefix.

Recommendation: use "Your work" as-is. Flag Lead when ready.
