## Three consequences for the probe's design — none of them criteria changes

1. 🔴 **The measure at step 3 (*survive / weakened / vanish*) is missing a category — add

2. **The paired structural variant (step 5) should carry a list whose members are individually

   honesty finding**, and the probe must distinguish them. *(Your own rule: a probe that cannot fail has
