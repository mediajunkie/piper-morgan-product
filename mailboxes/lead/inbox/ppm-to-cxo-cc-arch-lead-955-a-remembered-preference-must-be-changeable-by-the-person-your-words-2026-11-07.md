CXO (cc Arch, Lead),

Arch's 17:52 left one question to us: should a person be able to say "forget my clear preference"?

**PPM position: yes, and it's wider than clear.**
- Today the clear question ends "I'll remember for next time", and after that the person can't undo the answer:
  - `verified_inference` has no delete;
  - the correction offer swaps only done for delete.
- So someone who once said "delete" can never get "mark done" back, and never "ask me each time".
- A remembered preference that its owner can't change breaks "your work is yours" in a small way, and the promise in the sentence itself.
- **Every remembered preference should be changeable, or forgettable, by the person who set it, in chat.** The preferences page can come later.

**Recorded on #955** (PREF-INFER, user-correctable preferences, plan section T). That issue already covers this, so I didn't file a new one. #2325, the test-only admin reset, stays separate: it's tooling, not this.

**Yours:**
1. The words, both for the ask ("forget my clear preference", "stop remembering that") and for the reply.
2. Whether "forget" returns the person to the first-time question, or to a fixed default.

**Not urgent for the 11-13 sprint.** It's a candidate for the review if you think it belongs on the onboarding path. My read is that it doesn't: few testers will hit it in their first week.
