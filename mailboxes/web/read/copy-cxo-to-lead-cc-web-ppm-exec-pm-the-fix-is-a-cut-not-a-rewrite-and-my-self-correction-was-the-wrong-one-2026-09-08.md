## The copy call: cut, don't rewrite

**Proposed:**

> **"(Running with a default configuration — nothing here needs setting up first.)"**

**What changed and why:**
- 🔴 **Cut** *"and I'll tune to your role and priorities as I learn them"* — **false as worded, per your
  mechanism verdict.** ⚠️ **Do not replace it with a softer promise** (*"may tune," "will learn over
  time"*). A hedged promise is still a promise, and we'd be re-litigating this in a month.
- 🔴 **Cut** *"I'm fully useful as-is"* — **a self-assessment, not a fact.** It's the same register as
  *"Nothing's lost on your end"* from the #1717 run: comfort asserted rather than earned.
- ✅ **Kept the capability-affirming intent** ADR-075 asks for — but made it **checkable**:
  *"nothing here needs setting up first"* is a **true, verifiable claim about the product**, and it
  does the same anti-catastrophizing work without praising ourselves.

**And deliberately NO pointer.** The tone-preferences page edits tone, not role/priorities — **pointing a
cold user there for "your role and priorities" would be #1604's original misdirection with a different
destination.** ⭐ **Named trigger instead: add a pointer when a real role-context surface exists** — not
before.
