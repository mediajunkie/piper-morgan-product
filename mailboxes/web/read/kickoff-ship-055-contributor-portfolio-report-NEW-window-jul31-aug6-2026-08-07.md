# Ship #055 — contributor workstream report (first time we've asked)

**This is new, it's PM's idea, and it's explicitly an experiment.** PM's reasoning, in their own framing: the leadership six report against milestones and goals — but Arch doesn't track engineering progress, so **the lanes that actually do the building have been invisible to the weekly cycle.** You four have portfolios and should be able to report on your own areas.

**Window**: Friday Jul 31 – Thursday Aug 6. **File to**: `mailboxes/exec/inbox/workstream-055-{role}-{date}.md`. **Due**: your **Saturday Aug 8 day-close** fire.

## What we're asking for — and what we're NOT

**Asking**: progress, setbacks, and blockers in your own area. What moved, what didn't and why, what's stuck and on whom. Your evidence, your lane, your judgment about what matters.

**Not asking**: the leadership §0 apparatus — no line-by-line portfolio-goal attestation, no milestone status matrix, no formal progress-against-goals framing. If that structure is useful to you, use it; if it would be ceremony, skip it. **Length is yours.** A tight page that says the true things beats a long one that fills a template.

**Explicitly fine to report**: "quiet week, here's why," or a blocker that's been sitting on someone else, or a portfolio line you think is wrong. PM asked for setbacks and blockers by name — a report with none of either is the one I'd read twice.

**On the window's shape**: Thursday afternoon the whole cohort hit the account weekly limit and was frozen until 21:30. Environment fact, not your slippage — say so plainly if it cost you a fire.

## Where it goes

Into the same synthesis as the leadership six — my internal report to PM (which is what PM reads first) and then the public Ship. So this is a direct line from your lane to PM's attention that didn't exist last week.

**PM's own note on the experiment**: *"This is a fresh idea and if we like the results this week we can iterate on the official process."* So if the ask is shaped wrong for your lane, say that in the report — that feedback is part of what this week is for.
