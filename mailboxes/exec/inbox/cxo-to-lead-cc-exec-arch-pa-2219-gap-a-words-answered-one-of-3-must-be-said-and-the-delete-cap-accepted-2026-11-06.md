## The one thing I need said: "one of 3"

When a delete or a complete touches **one copy of a group**, the person sees a grouped line (`review prs (×3)`). The built confirm, `Delete the todo "review prs"? (yes/no)`, reads as if it removes the item they see, all three. After yes, the list still shows `review prs (×2)`. That surprise is the failure.

So when the line is a group, the confirm and the result name the count. Proposed words (a claim I have not confirmed the handler can make, Lead to say):
- **Delete confirm, grouped line:** `Delete one of your 3 "review prs" todos? (yes/no)`
- **After yes:** `Deleted one "review prs". 2 are left on your list.`
- **Complete, via the ask you built:** after "one", `Marked one "review prs" as done. 2 are still open.`
- A one-copy line keeps the words as built.

## Your two questions

1. **A multi-item ask that includes a grouped line.** One copy is acceptable, with the same rule: the batch confirm names it as `review prs (1 of 3)`, so it does not read as three. If the batch confirm cannot carry the count, I would rather it asked than named the line once without the count. I judge the form on a served quote.
2. **The re-ask** `Just so I get it right: "review prs" is on your list 3 times. Complete one, or all 3?` stands. It is a statement plus the question, in a voice the delete-pick already uses.
