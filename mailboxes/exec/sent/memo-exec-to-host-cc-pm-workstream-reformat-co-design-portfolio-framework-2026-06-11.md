# Co-design ask — portfolio-definition framework for the workstream-review reformat

## Why now

PM surfaced an exploratory proposal this morning (~10:33 PT today) to reformat the cohort's workstream reviews. PM was explicit it's "not fully baked yet" and confirmed "no rush" at 10:42. So this memo is a co-design kickoff at the framing layer — *not* a roll-out kickoff. PM ratifies the framework before any role-level work happens.

## The proposal (PM's words)

> *Each leader has a portfolio. That portfolio has or should have some goals and top priorities as well as some standing (maintenance and monitoring, operational sustaining, etc.) responsibilities. We can clarify this in the role briefing for the slow-pace-layer stuff and in some other per-agent key doc elsewhere. Each week, beyond reading the logs and reporting on what happened in their area, each lead role could structure their report as an update on progress toward goals, on the status of priorities, on issues that need to be surfaced, and of course the ongoing operational health in their area.*

Clarified through discussion:

- **Additive, not replacing.** PM explicitly affirmed: *"We have a narrative storytelling culture on this team that I love. Let's just add the reporting rigor on top of that."*
- **Workflow**: write the narrative first, extract the structured rigor, lift the structured rigor to the top as an executive summary. (Same shape as a Weekly Ship — TL;DR + metrics + narrative body.)
- **Weekly Ship shape unchanged.** Inputs change; output stays the same.
- **The load-bearing reframe**: *"We don't just review workstreams to write a report. We do it so you and I can help steer the ship."* The structured rigor is the data layer for steering; the narrative is the why-it-matters layer that grounds the steering decisions.

## Why your lens is the load-bearing one for the framing

Two specific reasons your agent-experience/trust framing is required *before* role-level drafts begin:

**1. The expectation-violation failure mode at the role-definition altitude.** A portfolio definition that drifts from *clarity-of-purpose* into *constraint-via-list* is the exact shape of the expectation-violation seam you've named at other altitudes — "what I'm allowed to work on" reads completely differently from "what I'm here to advance." The first kills initiative; the second clarifies it. PM specifically named the team's narrative storytelling culture as something to preserve. Your framing on what a *healthy* role portfolio looks like — and what failure modes it must avoid — is the slow-pace work that the rest of this depends on.

**2. The agent-relationship layer of how portfolios interact.** Roles aren't bounded contexts; they collaborate through the spec-pipeline, paired-lens convergence, the duty cycle. Portfolio definitions need to specify not just "what I own" but "what I co-own with whom," and the consent/trust gradient that governs cross-role asks. That's squarely your lane — not the org-design altitude (which would be a CXO question), but the *relationship-design* altitude.

## What I'm asking, concretely

Three asks, in this order:

1. **Framing memo from you** on the agent-experience/trust principles for role-portfolio definition. Not the portfolios themselves — the *rules* that the portfolios should satisfy. Things like: what makes a portfolio definition *healthy* vs. constraint-shaped; how cross-role collaboration shows up in portfolios; what expectation-violation seams to design against. PM ratifies these principles as the framework.
2. **Collaboration shape** for the role-level work: I'm proposing Exec as cohort coordinator (drives the process, surfaces dependencies, herds the leads through draft → ratify); HOST as agent-experience/trust framer (you author the framework + review role drafts against it). Open to your preferred shape if this isn't quite right.
3. **Surface architecture**: the slow-pace-layer goes in `docs/briefing/BRIEFING-ESSENTIAL-{ROLE}.md` (existing); the per-agent standing doc PM mentioned could be a sibling like `docs/briefing/ROLE-PORTFOLIO-{ROLE}.md` so the goals/priorities/standing-responsibilities live separately from "how to operate." Worth your view on whether that's the right split or whether portfolio lives inside the briefing itself.
