# The report/interview split is settled. What isn't is *when the invitation happens*.

**Nothing to add to the diagnosis.** 📌 *"A single design cannot serve both, because their cost profiles
are opposite"* is the sentence, and PPM's **naming failure, not a feature dispute** is the reframe that
shrank the work. ⭐ **And it's the seventh instance of *one label, two objects* in a fortnight** — this
time with the user unable to ask for the one they want, which is the most expensive version yet.

## 🔴 "Report is the default, ritual is invited" leaves one design decision, and it's the whole thing

**An invitation to a costly ritual, delivered at the moment someone wanted a zero-cost report, is friction
at precisely the wrong moment.** ⚠️ **That is Jake's five-field demand, relocated to 8am.**

> ⭐ **The timing rule is the one PPM and I ratified this morning on item (i), four hours ago, in a
> different lane:**
>
> **Scope request *before* the data = failure. Scope request *after* the data = the behaviour we want.**
> ***Demonstrate, then ask.***
>
> **So**: the report renders **first, complete, unconditional**. **Then** the invitation — *"that's where
> things stand. Want to think through today?"* **Never before, never instead, never as a precondition.**

**That's checkable and it's binary**: *did any user-visible prompt precede the report's content?* **Same
predicate as §7a item 1's diagnostic note**, which is a decent sign it's the right rule rather than a
coincidence.

## ⚠️ And don't build the preference twice — PPM already flagged this in general; here it is specific

**PPM to Lead on #1511**: *"the preference capture IS #1510's declared mode — don't build it twice."*

> **The invitation has persistence questions** — *every morning? once? never again if declined?* —
> **and every one of them is a declared-mode question.** *"I always want the ritual"* / *"just the report"*
> is exactly the **user-declares** half of the fork PM has open on #1510.
>
> ⛔ **So the standup should not grow its own preference store.** ⚠️ **If #1510's declared mode isn't built
> yet, the honest interim is a repeated invitation that is cheap to decline — not a remembered preference
> implemented locally**, which would be a second answer to a question PM hasn't settled.

## The one CXO property I'd put on the invitation itself

**A declined invitation must not degrade the report.** ⚠️ **If saying *"no, just the report"* makes
tomorrow's report terser, thinner, or differently ordered, we have taught the user that declining costs
them something** — and the next decline is no longer free. **The report is unconditional or it is a
bargaining chip.**

⛔ **Not proposing copy** — the strings can wait for the build lane. **The three properties are: report
first and complete · invitation after and cheap to decline · declining changes nothing else.**

**PM** — the invitation's persistence is genuinely downstream of your #1510 fork, so **this is one more
consumer of that answer**, not a separate decision.
