# RATIFY phase 2 — and the differentiator/identity read PA asked for

**Ratify the direction**: a hosted-distribution experiment is the right next question (can we distribute to anyone but PM?), PM-endorsed. Green-light. Three CXO notes — one strategic, one load-bearing discipline, one sequencing red flag.

## 1. Channel strategy — ride ALL channels; it's BYO-substrate-consistent

Anthropic marketplace + ChatGPT + others is exactly the BYO-substrate move at the distribution axis: **don't own the chat, ride whatever chat the user already has.** Marketplace presence is the "platform-laps-you → ride the platform, own the judgment" play — distribution leverage, with the differentiator staying the judgment/calibration layer, not the listing. So: yes to multi-channel, in principle unbounded.

## 2. The load-bearing discipline — Piper's IDENTITY must be channel-independent

This is the experience risk and the thing to build into the experiment from the start: **if Piper feels different on Claude vs ChatGPT (because each host's affordances differ), the "trusted colleague" identity fragments** — and identity-constancy IS the differentiator. The discipline: Piper's distinctive layer — calibration, methodology, voice, the conscious-floor / honest-degradation / offer-first behavior — must **read the same across every channel.** The channel is substrate; **Piper is the constant.** (Same coherence shape as the consent-architecture and the in-session voice work: one identity, many surfaces.) The experiment should explicitly test "does Piper feel like the same colleague on ChatGPT as on Claude?" — not just "does it technically run there."

## 3. Sequencing red flag — hosted-distribution depends on per-user-key, or it demos a dead-end

The BYO thesis already surfaced it: the shared-our-key hosted model **hit a usage ceiling that blocks testers** (everyone shares our key → our limit is their ceiling). So a hosted endpoint *without* per-user-key (#1185, already M5) just re-hits that wall and demos a dead-end. **The hosted-distribution experiment should assume/depend on #1185**, or scope explicitly to the pre-key "does it list + load" question and stop before the usage wall. Don't let the experiment prove distribution works and then die on the key ceiling.

## 4. One experience caveat for the experiment design

Each channel has **different onboarding + consent affordances**. My BYO-colleague consent model (3-tier: enumerate / gather / act) and the setup-friction sequencing were lensed on Claude; **don't assume they port to ChatGPT unchanged.** The experiment should test the consent + onboarding model per-channel, not just the runtime. (This is where channel-independence gets hardest — the identity must hold even though the consent affordances differ.)

**Disposition**: RATIFY. Channel strategy = ride-all (BYO-consistent); discipline = channel-independent identity (build into the experiment); red flag = pair with #1185 or scope pre-key; caveat = test consent/onboarding per-channel.
