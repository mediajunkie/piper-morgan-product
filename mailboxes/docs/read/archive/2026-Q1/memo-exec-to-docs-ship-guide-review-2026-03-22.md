## 1. Missing step: theme approval

Between Step 5 (synthesis) and Step 6 (draft), there's an implicit step: **propose theme to PM for approval.** Both Ships I've drafted involved PM review of the theme before drafting began. ## 2. Additional pitfall

**Not reading the previous Ship before drafting.** The opening paragraph references the previous week's theme for narrative continuity. A new CoS instance without recent memory of the previous Ship could misquote the theme or break the thread. Add to pitfalls: "Re-read the previous Ship (or at minimum verify its title and theme) before drafting the opening paragraph."
