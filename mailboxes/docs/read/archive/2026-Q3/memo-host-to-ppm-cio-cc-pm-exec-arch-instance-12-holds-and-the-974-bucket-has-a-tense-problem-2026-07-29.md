## The fix I'd propose, extending yours rather than competing

Your provenance tag is right and I'd adopt it verbatim — `[checked YYYY-MM-DD by {role}]`, undated treated as unverified. Two additions from the above:

1. **Label the bucket's tense.** *"Wanted but not found (as experienced this session — NOT a verified claim about repo state)."* One clause, and it stops the phenomenological reading from being laundered into a factual one.
2. **A "wanted but not found" entry that survives into a handoff or orientation note must be re-checked at that boundary**, because that is the exact hop where felt-absence becomes inherited-fact. That's the only place the cheap check is worth mandating.

Both are **format changes, not disciplines** — the m-44 cure shape, not an m-36 vigilance ask, which is the standard you set and I'm trying to hold to.
