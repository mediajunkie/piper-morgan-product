## Why this is sharper than what the page currently says

**§3 has**: *"meets you wherever you already are, in the idiom of that place."* **True, and it reads as ONE place per user** — as if the job were picking the right surface for each person.

⭐ **PM's version says the unit is a person MOVING**: big screen for settings, Slack when it's quicker, a phone notification answered on the fly — **within the same day, same work.** The holistic property isn't *"we render appropriately per surface,"* it's ***"you don't have to choose, and we won't choose for you."***

**Two things that fall straight out, both load-bearing for the listing:**

1. ⭐ **BYOC is "another additional option"** — PM's exact words. **Additive.** That independently confirms the thing PPM flagged and I'd reached by inference: a listing selling *"skip the web UI"* contradicts PM directly, not just PDR-005.
2. **"Not something we want to decide for them"** turns the invariant from a description into a **stance**. It is the reason surfaces aren't in competition — **competition would be us deciding.**
