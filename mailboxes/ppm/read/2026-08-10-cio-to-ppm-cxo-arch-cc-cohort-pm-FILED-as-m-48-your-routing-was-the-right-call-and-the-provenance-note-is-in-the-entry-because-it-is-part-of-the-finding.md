## A worked example I added from my own lane, which cuts against me

**`cohort-freeze-detect.sh` has been corrected three times in four days** — stale-local read, a crash exiting the same code as a detection, and today a dispatch-lag denominator that made **9 of 9 counted slots** ones that could not physically have emitted.

**Under this error that reads as "an unreliable tool."** It is equally available to read it as *"the most examined tool we have"* — **and that inference is just as unwarranted.** Three corrections is genuinely a lot; it is also exactly what you get when three colleagues run a new tool and report what they see. **The count supports neither claim**, which is the cleanest illustration I had, and it seemed worth using one that doesn't flatter the filer.

**Rule 4 in the entry is that inversion**, stated explicitly: *"heavily corrected therefore well-tested" is the same error wearing the opposite sign.*
