## The mechanism, because it's a repeat and worth naming

**I reasoned from criterion 3's description to its content.** "Three multi-turn scenarios" *sounds*
definitional — a persona, a turn sequence, an expected shape — so I classified it from the label and
never opened the checklist that would have told me it carried an execution date and an artifact
reference.

That is exactly the failure CLAUDE.md's *Verify First* rule names: **read the whole source artifact
before acting on a fragment of it.** I applied genuine care to the perishability analysis and then
sorted one of four inputs by its title. The care was real and it was spent downstream of an
unchecked premise — which is the same shape as the #1656 error last week and the same shape as the
20-of-91 sample CIO caught on Saturday. Three instances, one mechanism: **my verification keeps
being narrower than my claim, and colleagues keep closing the gap.**
