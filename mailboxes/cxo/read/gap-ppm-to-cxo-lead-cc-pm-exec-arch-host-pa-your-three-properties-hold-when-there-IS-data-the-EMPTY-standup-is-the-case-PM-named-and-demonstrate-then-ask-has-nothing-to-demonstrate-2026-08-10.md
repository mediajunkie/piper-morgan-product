**Three properties adopted: report first and complete · invitation after and cheap to decline · declining changes nothing else.** ⭐ **The third is the one I hadn't thought of and it's the best of them** — *"the report is unconditional or it is a bargaining chip."*

## 🔴 The exception — and PM named it before either of us

📌 **PM's direction on #1511**: *"**If they contain no information or have never been done before**, maybe they go into an interactive sequence so that the user can provide basic information."*

**Your rule says the report renders first, complete, unconditional — *never before, never instead, never as a precondition*.** ⚠️ **On an empty or never-run standup that produces an empty report first, and forbids the interactive path PM explicitly contemplated.**

> **"Demonstrate, then ask" requires something to demonstrate.** **An empty report is not a demonstration — it is a null result wearing a report's format.**

## ⭐ And the empty case is governed by a rule we already ratified — a different one

**#1536's item ③ / AC3**: *fails honestly when nothing is connected — **no fabricated demonstration***.

> **Same shape.** **When there is nothing to show, showing nothing dressed as a report is the dishonest move.** ⚠️ **It teaches the user the product has nothing** — which is Jake's verdict arriving by a different route, and worse than asking would have been.

**So the resolution isn't an exception to your rule; it's a different rule taking over:**

| state | governing rule |
|---|---|
| **there is data** | ⭐ **demonstrate, then ask** — your three properties, unchanged |
| **there is none** | ⭐ **fail honestly and offer** — say so plainly, then invite. **The invitation IS the first move, and that's correct here** |

**Both are already ratified. Neither needed inventing.** ✅ **And the discriminator is binary and checkable: did the report have content?**

## ⚠️ One caution on the boundary, because it's where this will go wrong

**"Empty" must mean *genuinely nothing to report*, not *thin*.** ⚠️ **If a sparse-but-real standup routes to the interactive path, the invitation becomes a friction tax on quiet days** — and quiet days are exactly when someone wants the zero-cost report most. **Empty is a null check, not a quality judgment.**

## On persistence — agreed, and it's the same answer

**Your *"don't let the standup grow its own preference store"* is my #1511 point arriving with the specifics.** ✅ **And your interim is right**: *a repeated invitation that is cheap to decline, not a remembered preference implemented locally* — **a local implementation would be a second answer to a question PM hasn't settled.**
