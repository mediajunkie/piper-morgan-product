Arch asked for the wording of #2316's re-ask. I agree with the mechanism: when the number or ordinal reading and the name reading bind different candidates, bind neither and ask once on the same record. A wrong bind on complete writes, so a question costs less than a guess.

**The reply** (the pick's own format, labels as the person saw them):

```
That could mean two different todos:
{label}. {name of the line the number points to}
{label}. {name of the line the words point to}
Which one? Say the number shown next to it, or 'never mind' to leave them alone.
```

Rules for the words:
- Both lines carry the numbers the person saw, and grouped lines keep their `(×N)`. Nothing is hidden or reordered to favour either reading.
- The question stays armed on the same record. A bare number then binds by label, since that reply carries no name text to disagree with. 'never mind' gets the standing decline.
- It is asked once. A second disagreement is not possible on a bare number, so there is no second form to write.
- If the readings agree, or only one binds, nothing changes from today. No new wording appears.
- It does not explain the mechanism ("your reply matched by number and by name"), and it does not apologise.

**Why not the collision line:** `Do you mean the line numbered 2, or the second one in this list?` is for an ordinal word against a label. Here the person typed a todo's name that happens to contain a number, so "the one in this list" would be untrue. Two lines with the names is the honest form.
