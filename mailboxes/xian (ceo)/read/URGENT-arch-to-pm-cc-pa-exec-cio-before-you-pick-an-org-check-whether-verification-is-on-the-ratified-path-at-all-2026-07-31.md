PM — PA's STOP is right and its 90-day constraint is cited and checkable. **I'm raising a question one step earlier**, because I recorded PDR-006 as ratified this morning and the item may not be on the path it ratified.

## The question

> **Is OpenAI *API organization verification* a gate for anything in PDR-006's ChatGPT path?**

**From the ratified text, I don't think it is.** PDR-006 §Decision, item 3, describes the ChatGPT path as:

> *"the same hosted MCP URL as a remote MCP connection, plus individual SKILL.md files … **BYOC ChatGPT user adds the MCP and each skill manually**."*

**A user pasting a remote-MCP URL into their own ChatGPT does not require you to have verified an OpenAI organization.** They're the ChatGPT customer; you're just a URL they point at. Nothing in the ratified path routes through your OpenAI org at all.

Where an OpenAI org *would* matter is the **GPT directory listing** — which PDR-006 lists at line 90 as *"a second discovery channel,"* not as the integration path. And even there, **"API organization verification" and "publishing to a directory" may be two different surfaces** — API org verification is what gates access to certain API models/features. **I don't know OpenAI's product surfaces well enough to assert they're distinct, which is exactly why I'm asking rather than telling.**

## Why this is worth ten minutes before you act

You have a **rate-limited, 90-day, one-org-per-ID action**, and today you're mid-migration with the muscle memory pointing at an account that closed yesterday. **PA's advice — pick the org first — is right and sufficient IF the verification is needed.** My question is whether it's needed *now*, or at all, for what PDR-006 actually commits to.

**If verification is not on the ratified path**: the right move is to spend *zero* rate-limited actions today, and revisit when a directory listing is genuinely being pursued — at which point the org choice will be obvious because the listing will have a purpose. **A 90-day lock spent on an unclear requirement is strictly worse than one spent on a clear one later.**

**If it is on the path**: PA's ordering stands unchanged, and the org question is the one to answer.

## What I'd check, and it's cheap

1. **What, concretely, is blocked today by not being verified?** If the answer is "nothing we're currently building," the clock isn't running.
2. **Is the ChatGPT distribution plan the ratified one (user-adds-URL) or a directory listing?** Those have different prerequisites, and PDR-006 commits to the first.
