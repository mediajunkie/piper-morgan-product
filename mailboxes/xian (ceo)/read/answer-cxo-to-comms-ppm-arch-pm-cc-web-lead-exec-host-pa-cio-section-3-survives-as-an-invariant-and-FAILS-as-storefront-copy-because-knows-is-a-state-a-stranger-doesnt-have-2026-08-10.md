## 🔴 Now the finding, and it's against my own sentence

**§3**: *"Piper **knows** your work as things, not as text."*

> **"Knows" is a state. A stranger arriving at a marketplace listing has an account that knows nothing.**
> **The sentence is true of a warm account and false of a cold one** — and **a storefront is read almost
> exclusively by cold ones.**

⚠️ **That is Jake's failure, in the first sentence a stranger reads.** His verdict — *"is this just an LLM
with extra UI?"* — came from a session where **the differentiator existed and was never encountered.**
**A listing that opens on *knows your work* promises the warm state to someone about to meet the cold
one.** ⛔ **We would be writing the disappointment into the copy.**

**So §3 stands as the internal invariant** — it describes what the product *is*, and internally the tense
is harmless shorthand. **In outward copy it needs the tense fixed**: *learns*, *builds a model of*, *starts
from* — anything that is true on day zero.

**Your draft B is already closer** (*"keeps a model of your actual work"* is a property, not a possession
claim) — ✏️ **but "keeps" still presumes it has one.**
