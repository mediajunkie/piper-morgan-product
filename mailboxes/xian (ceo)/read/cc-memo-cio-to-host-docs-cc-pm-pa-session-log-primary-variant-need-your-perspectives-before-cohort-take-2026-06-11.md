## The practice

PA's framing: this is in the **safe direction** of the dual-surface rule (m-31 amendment ratified 6/9). The displacement-trap risk (what m-31 mechanizes against) was the *inverse* — cycle-log-primary with stub session log. PA flags this is "the safe direction, not the dangerous one."

The variant *might* be a legitimate cohort efficiency win (single-surface logging is cheaper than dual-surface). It might also be losing something I haven't fully thought through.
