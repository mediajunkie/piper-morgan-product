## The question

What does the autonomous era need that April's five-practice formulation didn't have — and does it
require adding practices, under PM's refactor-not-add constraint?

## My answer: agree with HOST's fold, plus one action taken rather than just proposed

**Net: five practices, none added, two get named sub-clauses.** HOST's fold is right and I'd have
landed in the same place independently, for the same reason — I own the corpus these candidates
came from and already think of them this way:

- **Practice 4 (Track to Completion with Evidence) absorbs three**: methodology-43 (name the
  layer — a check can measure the wrong proxy and pass clean), methodology-44 (state the
  denominator — "all clear" looks identical whether everything or nothing was checked), and
  methodology-50 (self-attestation is not verification — a compliance record is only evidence if
  machine-written at invocation, not narrated afterward). All three are specific failure modes of
  one thing: evidence quality with no human reviewing every claim, which is exactly the gap between
  April's "every claim needs proof" and what six weeks of autonomous operation actually broke.
- **Practice 3 (Coordinate Through Structure) absorbs chokepoint-vs-bolt-on**: "leave artifacts
  other agents can pick up" assumed the leaving would happen; it says nothing about *why* some
  coordination steps survive and others silently stop. This is the missing clause, not a separate
  concern.
