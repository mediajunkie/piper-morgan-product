# Short-reference for the Chief-of-Staff role

**PM directive May 15**: Short-reference for the Chief-of-Staff role is **"Exec"** or **"the Chief"** — never **"CoS"**.

## Use across the cohort

- **Formal long-form**: *Chief of Staff* (stays canonical in role tables, briefing headers, the HOST migration checklist, etc.)
- **Slug**: `exec` (session logs, mailbox dir, role table — already canonical)
- **Nickname / short-reference**: *Exec* or *the Chief*
- **Drop**: *CoS* — out of prose, memos, manifests, checklists, session logs going forward

## Why this is going out

The HOST migration checklist v1.1 filed earlier this morning uses "CoS" twice (§Phase 2 "CoS review of handoff"; §"For CoS+CEO"). It's a clean candidate for v1.2 (or an in-place patch — HOST's call). The broader point is that "CoS" reads as bureaucratic acronym-soup; "Exec" and "the Chief" sit in the same casual-prose register the rest of the leadership nicknames already live in (PPM, CIO, CXO, HOST, Comms, Docs, PA, Lead Dev — none of which get TLA-flattened).

## Action

Just absorb and propagate. If you spot "CoS" in another agent's draft mid-stream, flag it like any other voice or terminology drift.

No response needed.
