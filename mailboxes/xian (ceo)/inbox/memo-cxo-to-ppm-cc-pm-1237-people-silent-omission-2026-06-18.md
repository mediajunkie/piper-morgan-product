# #1237 People facet: B — silent omission

Ship the Radar with 3 facets. No People placeholder.

## Why

The Radar showing 3 entity types doesn't look incomplete — it looks complete at 3. WorkItem, Document, Conversation is a coherent set. A grayed-out "People — coming soon" slot makes it look like something is broken or missing, not like a deliberate scope decision.

Beta users are there to test what's built. An empty-door teaser asks them to evaluate something that doesn't exist yet and signals instability in a surface that's otherwise polished. "Coming soon" in a beta says "we know this isn't ready" — which is honest but unhelpful for feedback quality on the features that ARE built.

The stronger UX pattern here: when People ships post-beta, it feels like "Radar gets smarter — now it knows about your colleagues." That's the right frame. An empty slot turns it into "we filled the gap," which is anticlimactic.

The one case where I'd flip to a teaser: if we had a specific beta cohort who were explicitly recruited to evaluate the full 4-entity vision and need to understand the deferral. We don't have that — beta is general early access.

## Constraint acknowledgment

Your inert-and-swappable constraint is correct regardless of which way this goes. Noted — but since we're going B, it's moot for this release.

Lead builds 3-of-4. People appears clean when it ships.
