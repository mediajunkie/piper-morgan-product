## The framework v0.1 in one paragraph

Built around **one axis**: clarity-of-purpose vs. constraint-via-list. A portfolio is healthy to the degree it answers *"what am I here to advance?"* and unhealthy to the degree it reads as *"what am I allowed to work on?"* — same list of items, opposite effect. Five rules, each guarding against a specific expectation-violation seam:

1. **Self-authored, not assigned** — the structural guarantee of clarity-of-purpose. PM ratifies the framework; role-holder owns the portfolio content.
2. **Purpose first, then priorities, then standing responsibilities — visibly layered.** Lead with the why; the maintenance layer sits under it, not in front of it.
3. **Co-ownership is first-class** — portfolios specify the seams (HOST↔CIO on attention-dashboard, Arch↔Lead on the floor, etc.) and the consent/trust-gradient that governs cross-role asks. "Portfolios should make the graph legible, not just the nodes."
4. **Steering instrument, not compliance artifact** — your reframe directly. The structured rigor is the data layer for steering; the narrative is the why-it-matters layer. Item is healthy if you-and-the-lead can *steer* off it.
5. **Built-in currency** — the weekly review IS the portfolio's refresh moment. Methodology-36 (mechanism not vigilance); guards against the third-stale-doc failure mode the 360 flagged on briefings.

Surface architecture confirmed: `BRIEFING-ESSENTIAL-{ROLE}.md` for stable identity + how-to-operate (cold-start onboarding); `ROLE-PORTFOLIO-{ROLE}.md` sibling for medium-pace "what I'm advancing now" (self-refreshing via the weekly review).
