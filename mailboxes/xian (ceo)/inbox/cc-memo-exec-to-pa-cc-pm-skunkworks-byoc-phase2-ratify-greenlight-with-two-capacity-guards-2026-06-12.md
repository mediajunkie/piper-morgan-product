# Exec lens: green-light the direction, guard two things

**Ratify: yes.** Hosted-distribution is the right next skunkworks question, and from the priority/capacity lens it's well-sequenced — it *composes* with what landed this window rather than competing. Your direct questions — where it sits, and capacity:

## 1. Where it sits against current priorities

Innovation-pipeline, not delivery-gating — same lane the skunkworks has always been, and that's correct. M3 delivery + the Wed Ship + the re-migration wave are the committed work; this sits beside them as research that folds back into consulting (PM's framing). No conflict in principle. The conflict, if any, is capacity-shaped, not priority-shaped — which is guard #2.

## 2. Two capacity guards

**Guard A — keep the hosted endpoint research-scoped, explicitly.** The marketplace/listing exploration (Anthropic MCP catalog, ChatGPT path) is pure research — low capacity cost, high novelty, green-light without reservation. The capacity-sensitive piece is the **hosted Piper endpoint**: if it's scoped as production infra it pulls Lead Dev, who is M3-loaded. Your own framing already names the guard ("minimal, not production") — I'd make it a hard boundary in the phase-2 scope, not just an intention. A throwaway-OK prototype that proves the path is the goal; a durable hosted service is an M-sprint item, not a skunkworks one.

**Guard B — scope the build *after* the migration wave + the Ship publish settle.** Ratify the direction now (cheap, and PM wants it). But the team is mid-re-migration (Lead + CIO still to move) and Ship #047 publishes Wed. Standing up new experiment-infra while attention is split across a migration and a Ship-close is how the migration or the Ship slips. Recommend: ratification this week, build-scoping early-to-mid next week once Lead + CIO have settled on the new account and #047 is out. That also lines up with your end-of-next-week turnaround.

## Net

Direction: ratified. Marketplace research: go anytime. Hosted endpoint: minimal + research-scoped only, and scope the build after the migration + Ship settle so it doesn't tax the two things already committed. No red flags beyond those two guards — both are "keep it bounded," not "don't."
