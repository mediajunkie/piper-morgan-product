# CXO IA: one Documents surface, "source" is a facet. /files isn't a page — it's a filter.

PM's model is right, and the duplication PM hit is the tell: **/documents and /files feel redundant because they ARE one concept** ("Files" = Documents filtered to source=uploaded). So the IA fix is the same move we've made twice now.

## The IA: consolidate; the distinguishing dimension is a facet, not a surface

**One Documents surface. `source ∈ {uploaded | generated | federated}` is a facet/filter within it — not a separate page.** "Files" stops being a page; it becomes (at most) a saved view "Documents · source: uploaded." This is the **exact same pattern** as:
- **Radar/history**: conversations are *one entity-type among others*, not a separate chat-list. (The history-sidebar flattened because it was a redundant slice given its own surface.)
- **#1264 palette**: don't mint a separate token for what's a slice of an existing one.

/documents-and-/files is the same shape: two surfaces for one concept where one is a source-slice. Two surfaces for one concept always reads as redundant (and the crossed empty-state copy is the symptom). Consolidate.

## Source isn't just organization — it carries provenance/trust (the load-bearing part)

The three sources have **different trust properties**, and the surface must show them honestly (same honest-provenance discipline as Radar + #1164):
- **Uploaded** — user brought it; owned, in our store. Baseline trust.
- **Generated** — *Piper produced it.* Must be **marked as Piper-generated** (agent-attribution honesty — don't blur Piper-authored with user-authored, same rule as the BYO-colleague actor_chain + the Radar provenance facet). A user must never mistake a Piper-generated doc for one they wrote.
- **Federated** — *referenced, not housed.* Different trust again: it lives elsewhere, so "is this current? who owns it?" differs — show its **source + freshness** honestly; never present a possibly-stale external reference as a fresh owned doc.

So `source` is a **provenance facet** (it's literally the same shape as the RadarEntity `provenance.source` + the Document Radar-source #1238), not just a filter chip. It drives a small honest badge ("Generated by Piper" / "Linked · external"), not only a filter. **PPM**: this is why source should be first-class on the Document model, with provenance semantics — it aligns with #1238 and the typed entity catalog.

## Design-for-all-sources, populate-as-they-land (the Radar pattern again)

Build the one Documents surface **source-aware now** (the facet + the three honest badges exist), and **populate per availability**: uploaded ships (it's today's /files); generated + federated surface as their backends land, with the empty-state teaching pattern until then ("Documents Piper generates will appear here"). Don't gate the *surface* on all three sources existing — that's the same lesson as the Radar 4-types: design the surface for the full model, fill it incrementally. (PPM's roadmap call on whether generated/federated are Beta or later; the IA holds either way.)

## Stage-4 trust-gate uniformity (Lead's Q)
Not uniform — it should **vary by source's trust property.** Owned-uploaded can be more permissive; federated/external warrants more caution (it's not ours to vouch for). So the trust-gate reads the `source` facet, same as everything else keys off it. (Mechanism = Arch/Lead; the principle is source-differentiated.)

## Migration + nav (#1268)
- Fix the crossed empty-state copy immediately (cheap; stops the active confusion).
- `/files` → folds into `/documents` as the source=uploaded default view; nav (#1268) shows **one "Documents"** entry, not two.
- Beta: ship the consolidated Documents surface with source-aware structure; uploaded populated; generated/federated empty-state-teaching until their backends land.
