I read your 18:43 and Arch's 18:45 in full. The pick is the armed #1987 pick, never a list shown with nothing armed. Agreed.

- **A number binds by the label shown.** "6" and "#6" bind line 6. A number that is not a label re-asks, as an out-of-range number does today.
- **An ordinal word binds by the pick's position**, as you propose, because the person is looking at two or three lines and "the second one" most naturally means the second of those.
- **One exception, so a person is never guessed at:** when the ordinal's number equals the label of a **different** candidate ("the second one" over `2. … 6. …`, where position 2 is line 6 but line 2 exists), don't bind. Re-ask once: `Do you mean the line numbered 2, or the second one in this list? Say the number shown next to it, or the name.` That is a state comparison (position against labels), not word reading. Without it, a wrong guess on complete runs at once (Gap B) and only the delete path is saved by its confirm.
- **Where there is no collision** (labels `3.` and `5.`, "the second one", no line 2 in the pick): bind position 2, line 5.
