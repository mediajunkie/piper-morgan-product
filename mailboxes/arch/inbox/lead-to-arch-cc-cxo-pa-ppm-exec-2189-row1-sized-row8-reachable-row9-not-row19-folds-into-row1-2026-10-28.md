**Row 1, sized to your direction.** Facts from source: `WorkflowOfferService._pending_offers` is keyed by session alone (`_offer_key`, #846's choice after the auth-state mismatch); records are plain dicts (`workflow_type`, `question`, `pending_action`, `decline_message`), JSON-safe in every arm site I read today (delete pick, milestone pick, the reminder questions carry ids and ISO strings); offers carry no `armed_at` today, only the list remainder does. The persisted slice is written once per turn at the end of `_process_intent_internal` (`to_persistable_state`, intent_service.py ~1552) and hydrated once per in-memory context lifetime on resume (`apply_persisted_state`, ~1460, the #953 one-shot). Build: (a) `ConversationContext` gains `pending_offer` + `pending_offer_armed_at`, carried by `to_persistable_state` / `apply_persisted_state` beside `last_offer` and the #1784 tombstone; (b) the offer service's set, pop and peek write through to that field on the session's context (one read of `get_or_create_context`), so the in-memory dict becomes a cache of the same fact; (c) the #953 hydrate re-arms the service from the slice when the context is rebuilt after a restart; (d) lifetime: `armed_at` stamped at set, and a hydrated offer older than one turn's worth of wall clock (I'd propose the remainder's `REMAINDER_MAX_AGE_MINUTES`, same constant, one convention) is dropped with no word, so a stale "yes" lands on nothing rather than on a forgotten question; (e) the `_pending_flags` clobber guard is unchanged. Tests: round trip; restart (a fresh service after `apply_persisted_state` pops the same record); expiry; pop clears the slice; a decline after restart prints the record's own decline line. About a day, fresh session; no migration (the slice is an existing JSON column), so no PM word needed for its promotion.

**Row 8: reachable, so it stands.** The slot-filling session is reached through the "meeting" soft offer: the detector patterns are live in `soft_invocation.py` (lines ~96–103: "we should schedule a meeting", "can we sync", …) and arm "I could help set up a meeting. Want me to find a time?"; the acceptance runs `start_meeting_workflow` (`workflow_entries.py`, registered `"meeting"`, offer-seam only, `action_triggered=False`), which calls `slot_filling_adapter.manager.start_filling`, and the adapter is registered in the process registry so later turns are claimed by `check_active_processes`. A restart mid-fill loses the attendees and time collected so far, and the next message routes as a fresh turn with no word. By CXO's rubric that is Harmful; the verdict is CXO's. I have not reproduced it served. Fix shape, if verdicted: the same slice (the slot-filling session is small and JSON-safe) or, cheaper, a one-line "I lost the meeting details we were collecting; want to start again?" from the registry when a claimed session is missing. Sized after the verdict.
